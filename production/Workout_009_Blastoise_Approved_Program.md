# WORKOUT 009 | Blastoise: Cannon Fortress
**Status:** Seven-exercise program and four moves creator-approved October 27, 2026, published as **Review**. A/B scripts are production drafts; not filmed; all four filmed Camera Tests remain pending.
**Level:** Advanced · **Duration:** approximately 65–75 min · **Working sets:** 22 · **Type:** Water.

## Pokémon research and training identity
Shellfish Pokémon, Water type. Larger body and two high-pressure cannons emerging from its shell. Its shell still protects, but now also houses powerful water artillery.
Two shoulder cannons, heavy forward drive, rotational water power, and load-bearing shell strength.
**Intensity:** Trap bar and incline bench around RPE 7–8; explosive outputs stay brisk and stop before speed or control declines.
**Equipment:** Two independent adjustable cable stacks, soft non-bouncing medicine ball and approved throw wall, trap bar, heavy sled, dumbbells, chest-supported bench, paired kettlebells.

## Official four-move signature core

| Pokémon move | Type | Real exercise | Dose | Story / biomechanics |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Hydro Cannon** | Water | Standing Dual-Cable Power Press | 3 × 5; rest 120 sec | Balanced two-handle chest-level forward blast with controlled acceleration and deceleration. The ultimate water starter cannon; allow real rest between sets. |
| **Wave Crash** | Water | Moderately Heavy Sled Push | 4 × 12–15 m; rest 120–150 sec | Sustained whole-body leg drive against substantial resistance. A much larger water charge than Aqua Jet, with no actual recoil or collision. |
| **Surf** | Water | Rotational Medicine Ball Scoop Toss to Wall | 3 × 4/side; rest 90–120 sec | Turn hips/feet and drive a soft ball across to a solid approved wall, making a wider and more powerful wave than Aqua Tail. |
| **Iron Defense** | Steel | Double-Kettlebell Front-Rack Carry | 3 × 20 m; rest 90–120 sec | Walk tall while maintaining a solid stacked torso under two held loads; shell-like whole-body tension under load. |

## Complete seven-exercise workout
**Warm-up:** 9–12 minutes: easy cardio 2–3 min; bodyweight hinge/squat, shoulder and scapular prep, hip-turn medicine ball rehearsal, light double-cable presses, empty front-rack carries, light sled lane walkthrough. Do 2–3 gradual deadlift ramp-up sets.

| Order | Signature move | Exercise | Work | Rest | Technique / intent |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Surf | **Rotational Medicine Ball Scoop Toss to Wall** | 3 × 4/side | 90–120 sec | Moderate soft ball, rotate from feet and hips, approved wall only, no bounce into faces. |
| 2 | Hydro Cannon | **Standing Dual-Cable Power Press** | 3 × 5 | 120 sec | Symmetrical pulleys, controlled brisk projection, decelerate both cables. |
| 3 | Supporting | **Trap Bar Deadlift** | 3 × 5 | 150 sec | RPE 7–8, no maximal pulls, brace and drive through full foot. |
| 4 | Wave Crash | **Moderately Heavy Sled Push** | 4 × 12–15 m | 120–150 sec | Strong horizontal drive on clear lane, not to failure. |
| 5 | Supporting | **Chest-Supported Dumbbell Row** | 3 × 8 | 90 sec | Controlled shoulder-blade motion and stable support. |
| 6 | Supporting | **Incline Dumbbell Bench Press** | 3 × 8 | 90–120 sec | Moderate incline, stable shoulder and wrist alignment, RPE 7–8. |
| 7 | Iron Defense | **Double-Kettlebell Front-Rack Carry** | 3 × 20 m | 90–120 sec | Stand tall with stacked ribs; controlled steady walking, no breath-holding race. |

**Cooldown:** 3–5 minutes easy walk, breathing, gentle shoulder and hip mobility.

## Level modifications
- **Beginner:** No rotational throwing: use a light standing band rotational press. Light band two-arm chest press instead of dual-cable power press. Light sled march or push 2–3 × 10m, elevated kettlebell pull, light row, easy incline press and one light goblet carry.
- **Intermediate:** Half-kneeling cable presses with moderate control, medball wall pass at low load, moderate sled, lighter front-rack pair or single goblet carry, deadlift at RPE 6–7.
- **Advanced:** Follow the 22-set prescription; increment sled/cable/carry load gradually only when output and recovery remain consistent. No max effort or stacked fatigue into throws.

## Safety and load progression
Clear room around cable handles and set both towers evenly; never let weight stacks slam back. Use only a gym-approved solid wall, safe ball and empty throw lane for rotational tosses. Keep medicine-ball speed subordinate to foot/hip rotation; no forced lumbar twisting. Heavy sled path clear; no recoil collisions. Paired front-rack loads are optional for those with shoulder limitations; use a single light goblet carry. No actual water or slippery flooring.
Log the exercise, load, distance and technique quality. Progress one variable at a time across sessions; do not force all three evolutionary workouts into a single day. Power sets end when speed or form decreases. Stop for pain or dizziness.
