# Workout #015 — Beedrill: Double Sting

**Status: Creator-approved October 27, 2026.** Exercises and matched A/B scripts published as **Review**. Filming, four actual Camera Tests and A/B results pending; not Final.

## Pokémon research and training identity

- **National Pokédex:** #015 · **Type:** Bug / Poison · **Stage:** Final · **Counterpart:** #012 Butterfree.
- **Lore and ability:** Poison Bee Pokémon, Bug/Poison type; Swarm and hidden Sniper. Two arm stingers, fast dive-in strikes and physical Attack focus distinguish it from Butterfree’s status/special fighting style.
- **Relationship:** The opponent to Butterfree’s Sleep Powder. Twineedle is the anime Beedrill attack that Metapod intercepts before evolving.
- **Workout goal:** Safe explosive entry, alternating linear striking mechanics, lower-body force and controlled slashing under load.
- **Level:** Advanced · **Planned duration:** 60–70 minutes including setup/rest/transitions. · **Intensity:** Front squat and presses at RPE 7–8; sled and fast-looking attacks stop when speed or form slips.
- **Equipment:** Unobstructed sled lane, properly anchored landmine, independent light cable stacks, front squat rack with safeties, dumbbells, flat bench, chest-supported bench.
- **Evolution chain:** Weedle → Kakuna → Beedrill. **Opposing line:** Caterpie → Metapod → Butterfree.

## Complete seven-exercise workout

| Order | Move | Real exercise | Sets × dose | Rest | Key coaching cue |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fell Stinger | Sled Push Acceleration | **3 × 12 m** | 120 sec | Sled light enough to accelerate crisply with a clear 12 m lane; stop before grinding. |
| 2 | Poison Jab | Single-Arm Landmine Push Press | **3 × 5/side** | 120 sec | Secure landmine base, start at shoulder, initiate with slight leg drive and controlled overhead-forward press. |
| 3 | Supporting | Barbell Front Squat | **3 × 5** | 150 sec | Progressive warm-up sets first, upright brace and foot pressure; RPE 7–8 without grinding. |
| 4 | Twineedle | Standing Alternating Cable Punch | **3 × 6/side** | 90 sec | Very light independently adjusted cables; alternate chest-height pushes with slight elbow bend at reach. |
| 5 | Supporting | Chest-Supported Dumbbell Row | **3 × 8** | 90 sec | Stable chest support, no torso heave. |
| 6 | Supporting | Flat Dumbbell Bench Press | **3 × 8** | 90 sec | Shoulder blades stable and comfortable elbow path, use spotter when appropriate. |
| 7 | Swords Dance | Standing Two-Handed Cable Diagonal Chop | **3 × 8/side** | 90 sec | Sweep high to low across torso using a gentle hip/foot pivot, then control cable return. |

**Total:** 21 prescribed sets across seven exercises. **Signature-move sets:** 12. The sets shown are training prescriptions; additional warm-up rehearsals are not included.

### Warm-up

9–11 min light cardio and range of motion, 8 bodyweight squats, 8 hinges, scapular work, light two-cable punches, empty landmine press patterns, a light sled pass. Add 2–3 ramp-up front-squat sets outside working sets.

### Cooldown

3–5 min gentle walking, easy breathing and relaxed shoulder/wrist/hip mobility.

### Regressions / progressions

- Beginner: light slow band alternating presses, planted light single-arm landmine press, bodyweight box squat, easy light sled march, chest-supported row, wall push-up and very light band diagonal sweep. Do not follow full advanced 21-set volume.
- Intermediate: smaller work doses (2 sets for each signature and support), front squat RPE 6–7, no maximal velocity on punches or diagonal chops.
- Advanced: full 21-set session, RPE 7–8 on strength, maintain speed on power drills with full recovery and clean filmed movement.

### Equipment and safety checks

Never punch people or pads with loaded cables, or snap elbows into lockout. Keep each cable stack light and symmetric; wear closed shoes and control handle returns. Secure the landmine and keep the path/ceiling clear. Sled route must be empty; no real stingers, toxins or contact combat. Cable chops rotate through controlled feet and hips, not aggressive lumbar twisting.

- Keep signature technique full-body and legible with **no special effects** on the first Camera Test; a clean filming rehearsal is possible but has not happened yet.
- Work on stable dry flooring; provide full recovery between faster or power movements. The entire session need not be completed by beginners if they require regressions.
- This is fitness-inspired entertainment, not real contact combat or a guarantee of effects from a Pokémon status move.
