# Workout 002: Ivysaur — Rooted Power

**Status:** Seven-exercise workout approved on October 21, 2026; videos and camera tests pending.  
**Identity:** Intermediate, strength + power + stability; about 45–55 min.  
**Production concept:** “So You're Training Ivysaur?” Viewer is the trainer; GymTrainerMax addresses them directly. Professor Oak appears only in Workout 000.  
**A/B principle:** Shared camera footage and editing, two dialogue tracks and corresponding subtitle tracks.  
**Target:** ~68 seconds, 9:16 vertical.  

### Canonical four-move core

| Official move | Type | Fitness mapping | Training prescription |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sludge Bomb | Poison | Rotational Medicine Ball Wall Throw | 2 × 5/side, 90-sec rest |
| Growth | Normal | Dumbbell Thruster | 3 × 6–8, 90–120-sec rest |
| Grassy Glide | Grass | Lateral Slider Lunge | 2 × 8/side, 75-sec rest |
| Synthesis | Grass | 90/90 Diaphragmatic Breathing | 2 × 5 slow breaths, comfortable pace |

Source check: Ivysaur learns all four in *Scarlet/Violet* (Growth and Synthesis via level-up; Sludge Bomb TM148; Grassy Glide TM194). Grassy Glide gets priority only with Grassy Terrain. Synthesis restores Pokémon HP in-game; breathwork is a practical recovery drill, not literal healing. Reference: https://pokemondb.net/pokedex/ivysaur/moves/9 and https://pokemondb.net/move/synthesis

### Approved full workout (unchanged)

**Warm-up 7–8 minutes:** Light cardio, unloaded squats/hinges, shoulder preparation, lateral steps, and light rotational throw rehearsals.

| Order | Exercise | Sets / reps | Rest |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Sludge Bomb** — Rotational Medicine Ball Wall Throw | 2 × 5/side | 90 sec |
| 2 | Trap Bar Deadlift | 3 × 6 | 120 sec |
| 3 | **Growth** — Dumbbell Thruster | 3 × 6–8 | 90–120 sec |
| 4 | Incline Dumbbell Bench Press | 2 × 8–10 | 75–90 sec |
| 5 | Chest-Supported Dumbbell Row | 3 × 10 | 75–90 sec |
| 6 | **Grassy Glide** — Lateral Slider Lunge | 2 × 8/side | 75 sec |
| 7 | **Synthesis** — 90/90 Diaphragmatic Breathing | 2 × 5 breaths | Comfortable pace |

**Cooldown 3–5 min total:** Synthesis starts the cooldown; then easy walking / gentle mobility. Main strength sets approximately RPE 7–8, thrusters and slides 6–7; throws crisp and submaximal. Avoid invented medical promises about Synthesis.
