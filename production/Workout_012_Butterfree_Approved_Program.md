# Workout #012 — Butterfree: Wing Control

**Status: Creator-approved October 27, 2026.** Exercises and matched A/B scripts published as **Review**. Filming, four actual Camera Tests and A/B results pending; not Final.

## Pokémon research and training identity

- **National Pokédex:** #012 · **Type:** Bug / Flying · **Stage:** Final · **Counterpart:** #015 Beedrill.
- **Lore and ability:** Butterfly Pokémon, Bug/Flying type; Compound Eyes and hidden Tinted Lens. Large wings, special attack orientation and powders make smooth control and non-contact tactics a natural counterpart to Beedrill’s physically directed moves.
- **Relationship:** Final forest confrontation: Butterfree uses controlled wing movement and Sleep Powder while Beedrill relies on Twineedle strikes. In the anime, Butterfree’s powder ends the pursuing Beedrill swarm.
- **Workout goal:** Smooth coordination, upper-back wing control, rotational cable power, rhythmic conditioning and a non-magical downshift.
- **Level:** Intermediate · **Planned duration:** 50–60 minutes including setup/rest/transitions. · **Intensity:** Strength RPE 7 with 2–3 reps in reserve. Rope speed never overrides posture. Sleep Powder is recovery work, not loaded strength.
- **Equipment:** Light long resistance band, cable column, battle ropes, light dumbbells, chest-supported bench, incline bench, mat and optional bench for calves.
- **Evolution chain:** Caterpie → Metapod → Butterfree. **Opposing line:** Weedle → Kakuna → Beedrill.

**Move-set uniqueness:** Four distinct moves within the episode. Their unordered four-move combination is unique against all published workouts #001–009 and these proposed #010–015 combinations. ## Complete seven-exercise workout

| Order | Move | Real exercise | Sets × dose | Rest | Key coaching cue |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Quiver Dance | Lateral Step with Resistance-Band Pull-Apart | **3 × 8 steps/side** | 75 sec | Light tension only, arms open as side step lands; control rather than yank the scapulae. |
| 2 | Air Slash | Half-Kneeling Single-Arm High-to-Low Cable Sweep | **3 × 8/side** | 75–90 sec | Use low-to-moderate resistance, sweep from above shoulder toward opposite pocket with torso centered. |
| 3 | Supporting | Reverse Lunge | **3 × 8/side** | 90 sec | Controlled back step and strong stable front foot; use weights only if balanced. |
| 4 | Bug Buzz | Alternating Battle Rope Waves | **3 × 20 sec** | 90 sec | Keep trunk steady, alternate quick even rope pulses, no bouncing to compensate. |
| 5 | Supporting | Chest-Supported Dumbbell Row | **3 × 8** | 90 sec | Row toward back pockets with shoulder blades smoothly retracting. |
| 6 | Supporting | Incline Dumbbell Bench Press | **3 × 8** | 90 sec | 15–30 degree incline, smooth lowering, stop before shoulder strain. |
| 7 | Sleep Powder | 90/90 Diaphragmatic Breathing | **2 × 5 relaxed breaths** | 45 sec | On back, calves on bench and hips/knees around 90 degrees; comfortable quiet breathing without forced breath holds. |

**Total:** 20 prescribed rounds across seven exercises: **18 movement/training sets plus 2 easy recovery-breathing rounds** (Sleep Powder). **Signature-move prescriptions:** 11 rounds, including breathing. The two breathing rounds are **not working strength sets**, and the recovery reset goes at the end.

### Warm-up

8–10 min: easy bike or walk, upper-back rotations, 8 unweighted lunges, light band pull-apart rehearsals, very light cable sweep and 8-sec low-amplitude rope waves.

### Cooldown

3–5 min easy walk and relaxed shoulder/hip movements following breathing reset.

### Regressions / progressions

- Beginner: side step without band, slow unweighted arm sweep or band sweep, supported reverse lunge, soft ropes for 10 sec, lighter rows and presses, or sit comfortably for breathing.
- Intermediate: listed doses and RPE around 7, preserve smooth coordination and calm breathing.
- Advanced: add modest resistance to row/press and increase precision; keep rope bouts short and breathing truly easy.

### Equipment and safety checks

No powders, aerosols, fragrances, smoke or substances are used to simulate Sleep Powder. All particles are visual effects. Set ropes in a clear bay, inspect anchors and cables. Keep feet planted during cable sweeps instead of wrenching the spine. Sleep Powder does not imply medical sleep treatment or sedation.

- Keep signature technique full-body and legible with **no special effects** on the first Camera Test; a clean filming rehearsal is possible but has not happened yet.
- Work on stable dry flooring; provide full recovery between faster or power movements. The entire session need not be completed by beginners if they require regressions.
- This is fitness-inspired entertainment, not real contact combat or a guarantee of effects from a Pokémon status move.
