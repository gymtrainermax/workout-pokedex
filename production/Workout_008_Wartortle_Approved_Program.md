# WORKOUT 008 | Wartortle: Current Control
**Status:** Seven-exercise program and four moves creator-approved October 27, 2026, published as **Review**. A/B scripts are production drafts; not filmed; all four filmed Camera Tests remain pending.
**Level:** Intermediate · **Duration:** approximately 50–60 min · **Working sets:** 18 · **Type:** Water.

## Pokémon research and training identity
Turtle Pokémon, Water type; its large ears and fluffy tail function as swimming balance and steering aids. This stage trades some of Squirtle’s simplicity for stability and more powerful motion.
More speed, balance and control. Tail-like sweeping power and defense against outside resistance.
**Intensity:** Deadlift/rows/lunges at RPE 7–8, other moves moderate and technique-first.
**Equipment:** Sled and clear lane, cable column, secured landmine, trap bar, dumbbells, bench or row support.

## Official four-move signature core

| Pokémon move | Type | Real exercise | Dose | Story / biomechanics |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Aqua Jet** | Water | Light Sled Push (Acceleration Focus) | 3 × 12 m; rest 90–120 sec | Drive faster than the Squirtle sled march, without overloading. Brisk priority attack that still ends under control. |
| **Water Pulse** | Water | Half-Kneeling Single-Arm Cable Press | 3 × 8/side; rest 75–90 sec | Press forward from the ribcage while the torso resists cable rotation. More pressure and steadiness than the band Water Gun. |

## Complete seven-exercise workout
**Warm-up:** 8–10 minutes: 2–3 min light cardio; bodyweight lunges and hip hinges; gentle hip/foot pivots; light cable press and Pallof practice; unloaded landmine 180s and light sled pass. Take 2–3 gradual trap-bar ramp-up sets.

| Order | Signature move | Exercise | Work | Rest | Technique / intent |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Aqua Jet | **Light Sled Push (Acceleration Focus)** | 3 × 12 m | 90–120 sec | Light enough to accelerate quickly; stop before speed fades. |
| 2 | Supporting | **Trap Bar Deadlift** | 3 × 5–6 | 120–150 sec | RPE 7–8, braced setup, no grinding. Ramp-up sets first. |
| 3 | Water Pulse | **Half-Kneeling Single-Arm Cable Press** | 3 × 8/side | 75–90 sec | Front knee planted, glute engaged on down knee, press straight forward. |
| 4 | Aqua Tail | **Landmine 180s** | 3 × 8 alternating sweeps total | 75–90 sec | Controlled sweep with hips/feet rotating, no wrenching the lumbar spine. |
| 5 | Supporting | **Reverse Lunge** | 2 × 8/side | 75–90 sec | Long enough stance to balance; bend both knees comfortably. |
| 6 | Supporting | **Chest-Supported Dumbbell Row** | 2 × 8–10 | 90 sec | Maintain chest support; use a load that permits full control. |
| 7 | Protect | **Tall-Kneeling Pallof Press** | 2 × 8/side, 2-sec hold | 75 sec | Press and pause; pelvis and chest stay facing forward. |

**Cooldown:** 3–5 minutes easy walking, calm breathing and comfortable hips, mid-back and shoulder mobility.

## Level modifications
- **Beginner:** Light marching sled rather than speed push; cable press at lighter load or standing band press; light two-hand landmine arc or band rotation; low cable kneeling Pallof hold; elevated kettlebell pull and split squat.
- **Intermediate:** Use listed doses; deadlift at RPE 7–8, then maintain control on all anti-rotation and sweeping work.
- **Advanced:** Add a small amount of cable/landmine/sled resistance only if speed, balance and the footage stay crisp; no ballistic lumbar spins.

## Safety and load progression
Aqua Jet is not a treadmill sprint. Set a clear sled lane. Make sure the landmine sleeve is securely anchored and keep bar clear of other people. Rotate feet and hips naturally; protect knees and back. A Pallof press is controlled anti-rotation, not a literal force field.
Log the exercise, load, distance and technique quality. Progress one variable at a time across sessions; do not force all three evolutionary workouts into a single day. Power sets end when speed or form decreases. Stop for pain or dizziness.
