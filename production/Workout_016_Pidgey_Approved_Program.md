# Workout #016 — Pidgey: Route One Flight School
**STATUS: Creator-approved program and A/B script for public Review, October 27, 2026. Filming and four Camera Tests pending; not Final.**

## Pokémon research profile
- **National Pokédex:** #016
- **Type:** Normal / Flying
- **Stage:** First-stage / learn to take off
- **Anatomy and character:** Tiny Bird Pokémon; Normal/Flying. Uses low wingbeats to disturb sand, avoids needless fights, and navigates accurately back to its nest. Evolution: Pidgeotto at level 18.
- **Workout identity:** Basic acceleration, small wing-driven air movements, careful footwork, and foundational pushing and pulling.
- **Level and intensity:** Beginner+; RPE 6–7 for resistance work, fast drills at submaximal effort
- **Estimated duration:** 40–50 minutes including rests, setup and transitions.
- **Equipment:** Unobstructed 10 m gym lane; light sled; two light battle ropes with rated floor anchor; securely anchored light resistance band; dumbbell/kettlebell; dumbbells; incline bench.

## Complete seven-exercise workout
**Programming:** Work in this order, regardless of the 70-second reel edit. Working sets do not include ramp sets, warm-up drills, or cooldown. Keep a comfortable reserve and stop speed work when control slips.

| Order | Pokémon move | Real gym exercise | Sets × dose | Rest | Coaching cue |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Quick Attack | Short Acceleration Run from Split Stance | 3 × 10 m | 75–90 sec | Start with one foot forward, accelerate for 10 m, decelerate in a marked lane; no max sprinting. |
| 2 | Gust | Alternating Battle Rope Waves | 2 × 15 sec | 75 sec | Soft knees and small, rhythmic waves; stop before shoulder shrugging starts. |
| 3 | Tackle | Light Sled Push | 3 × 10 m | 90 sec | Neutral neck, ribs stacked, drive with short deliberate steps; use a clear lane. |
| 4 | Sand Attack | Standing Single-Arm Low-to-High Resistance-Band Sweep | 2 × 8/side | 60–75 sec | Use a fixed low rated anchor, smooth up/out diagonal, controlled return, no twisting at low back. |
| 5 | Supporting | Goblet Squat | 2 × 8–10 | 90 sec | Whole foot planted, comfortable depth, light RPE 6–7. |
| 6 | Supporting | Chest-Supported Dumbbell Row | 2 × 10 | 75 sec | Keep chest supported and pause briefly at the top. |
| 7 | Supporting | Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift | 2 × 8–10 | 90 sec | Soft knees, hinge back with dumbbells close, stop before spinal rounding. |

**Session volume:** 16 prescribed sets (all working sets).

### Warm-up
7–8 minutes: 2 minutes of easy cycling or walking; 8 air squats, 8 unloaded hinges, 10 gentle arm circles; rehearse one half-speed acceleration, an unloaded sled pass, easy rope waves and light band sweeps. Warm-up work is not included in the set count.

### Cooldown
3–5 minutes easy walking, comfortable shoulder and hip mobility, relaxed nasal breathing.

## Beginner / intermediate / advanced modifications
- **Beginner:** Reduce acceleration to 5 m or brisk walk; empty or barely loaded sled; low rope amplitude; lighter band; use bench-supported box squats; reduce hinge range.
- **Intermediate:** Progress one resistance or distance slightly while maintaining clean acceleration and stop mechanics; keep the battle-rope waves brief.
- **Advanced:** Treat as technique/recovery day, not a maximum sprint or fatigue challenge. Add load in a separate strength session, not by distorting this episode.

## Equipment and safety
Use slip-free shoes, a clear 10 m acceleration lane and marked deceleration space. Never collide with people or objects. Secure ropes and resistance bands to rated anchors; inspect attachments before recording. Do not kick real sand indoors; the visual dust is VFX only. Power and speed stop when coordination deteriorates. Avoid spinal rotation without whole-body pivot.

## Same-name move continuity audit
- **Tackle (Pidgey):** Light sled push. Exactly the established forward sled family already seen with Bulbasaur, Squirtle, Caterpie and Metapod. It is not a crawl or a body collision.
- **Air Slash (Pidgeot):** Half-kneeling single-arm high-to-low cable sweep. Same real exercise and silhouette used for Butterfree’s Air Slash; a different difficulty does not redefine the attack.
- **Gust → Whirlwind → Hurricane:** A planned continuous rope progression: small alternating waves, outward circles, bigger deliberate double-arm circles. These are three *different* game moves, not canonical move upgrades.
- **Quick Attack → Agility → Tailwind:** Short acceleration, reactive footwork and resisted lateral steps. Tailwind is a team-speed status move in the games, not a literal gym performance guarantee.
- **Sand Attack → Wing Attack → Air Slash:** A light diagonal wing/sand sweep develops into a cable-driven wing path, then a precise half-kneeling high-to-low sweep.
- **Tackle → Pidgeotto trap-bar bridge → Brave Bird:** The forward-power theme begins with a sled push, builds hip/leg strength at stage two and culminates in a powerful sled drive. Pidgeotto's deadlift is a *supporting* exercise, not an invented move.
