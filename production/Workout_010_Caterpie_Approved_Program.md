# Workout #010 — Caterpie: Silk School

**Status: Creator-approved October 27, 2026.** Exercises and matched A/B scripts published as **Review**. Filming, four actual Camera Tests and A/B results pending; not Final.

## Pokémon research and training identity

- **National Pokédex:** #010 · **Type:** Bug · **Stage:** Larva · **Counterpart:** #013 Weedle.
- **Lore and ability:** Worm Pokémon, Bug type; small grasping feet and silk-producing mouth. Shield Dust, hidden Run Away. Squishy basic locomotion and silk control before cocoon defense.
- **Relationship:** Caterpie starts the soft, controlled side of the rivalry. Weedle learns similar thread and bite skills but directs them toward precise sting attacks.
- **Workout goal:** Introductory sled propulsion, silk projection, basic pulling/hinging, and brief rope conditioning.
- **Level:** Beginner+ · **Planned duration:** 40–50 minutes including setup/rest/transitions. · **Intensity:** Technique-first. Most strength sets RPE 6–7. Leave at least 3 reps in reserve.
- **Equipment:** Clear non-slip floor, rated resistance-band anchor, light band, low-load soft sandbag, battle ropes, light dumbbells, goblet weight, supported-row bench.
- **Evolution chain:** Caterpie → Metapod → Butterfree. **Opposing line:** Weedle → Kakuna → Beedrill.

## Complete seven-exercise workout

| Order | Move | Real exercise | Sets × dose | Rest | Key coaching cue |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tackle | Low-Stance Light Sled Push | **3 × 8 m** | 75 sec | Use high or low sled handles for a comfortable forward lean; take tiny slow steps, keep hips level, and never crawl beneath the sled. |
| 2 | String Shot | Standing Single-Arm Resistance-Band Press | **2 × 10/side** | 60–75 sec | Anchor band behind at chest height and press forward with stacked ribs; no uncontrolled recoil. |
| 3 | Supporting | Goblet Squat | **3 × 8** | 90 sec | Whole foot stays grounded; comfortable depth, smooth standing. |
| 4 | Bug Bite | Sandbag Bear-Hug Hold | **2 × 20 sec** | 60 sec | Hug a soft light sandbag, breathe normally; no crush-grip challenge. |
| 5 | Supporting | Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift | **2 × 8–10** | 90 sec | Hinge at hips with slight knee bend and neutral trunk; stop before back rounds. |
| 6 | Supporting | Chest-Supported Dumbbell Row | **2 × 10** | 75 sec | Keep chest on bench, pull through the elbows, then lower slowly. |
| 7 | Electroweb | Alternating Battle Rope Waves | **2 × 15 sec** | 75 sec | Soft knees and small fast rope ripples; do not thrash the back. |

**Total:** 16 prescribed sets across seven exercises. **Signature-move sets:** 9. The sets shown are training prescriptions; additional warm-up rehearsals are not included.

### Warm-up

7–8 min: easy walk 2 min, 8 air squats, 8 unloaded hip hinges, wrist/shoulder circles, low-stance empty-sled push rehearsal, five unresisted single-arm presses and gentle rope pulses.

### Cooldown

3–5 min easy walk and comfortable shoulder, wrist and hip mobility.

### Regressions / progressions

- Beginner: use an empty sled with higher handles, shorten to 4 m or rehearse the sled-push stance against a stable wall; feather-light band; empty-bag hug; skip ropes in favor of 10-sec small rope waves or low-effort band pulls.
- Intermediate: modestly increase band/sandbag load and maintain smooth goblet squat/hinge at RPE 7.
- Advanced: keep this as technical coordination day; add precision and not maximal weight.

### Equipment and safety checks

Secure bands to rated immovable anchors and examine for damage. Keep hands on non-slip flooring and avoid uncomfortable wrist loading while gripping sled handles. Clear rope zone and never use real electricity, cords tied to people or strobe effects.

- Keep signature technique full-body and legible with **no special effects** on the first Camera Test; a clean filming rehearsal is possible but has not happened yet.
- Work on stable dry flooring; provide full recovery between faster or power movements. The entire session need not be completed by beginners if they require regressions.
- This is fitness-inspired entertainment, not real contact combat or a guarantee of effects from a Pokémon status move.
