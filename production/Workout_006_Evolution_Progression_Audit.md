# Workout 006 — Charizard: Evolution Progression Audit (v2)

**Creator-selected official moves:** Dragon Claw, Seismic Toss, Flare Blitz, Blast Burn. These stay fixed. **Status:** Creator-approved evolved program published in Review, not Final; all four filmed Camera Tests pending. Prior approved #004/#005 programs remain untouched.

## The three inherited move chains

| Story chain | #004 Charmander (approved) | #005 Charmeleon (approved) | #006 Charizard (v2 proposal) | Progression test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Scratch → Slash → Dragon Claw | Single-Arm High-to-Low Diagonal Cable Pulldown, 2 × 8–10/side | Two-Handed High-to-Low Cable Woodchop, 2 × 8/side | **Single-Arm High-to-Low Cable Chop with Controlled Step-and-Pivot**, 3 × 8/side | Same descending slash direction; progresses isolated pulling → coordinated rotation → unilateral dynamic footwork; must be identifiable without VFX |
| Flame Charge → Flare Blitz | Short Acceleration Run, 3 × 10–15 m | Trap Bar Deadlift 3 × 5–6 and Reverse Lunge 2 × 8/side build the leg strength bridge, but there is no identical signature charge move in #005 | **Sled Push**, 4 × 12–15 m | Same continuous horizontal drive, now externally resisted; stop the set if forward speed collapses |
| Ember → Flamethrower → Blast Burn | Medicine Ball Chest Pass to Wall, 3 × 5 | Standing Svend Press, 3 × 8–10 | **Two-Handed Landmine Thruster**, 3 × 5 | Chest-height forward attack becomes leg-driven press forward/up; giant single explosion VFX only at press finish; must not resemble generic squat footage |

## Seismic Toss has no move predecessor

**Charizard:** Sandbag Over-the-Shoulder Toss, 3 × 4 total alternating shoulders. New character identity: aerial grappling / full-body opponent throw. It has a *strength foundation* in Charmander's Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift and Charmeleon's Trap Bar Deadlift, but there is no official Scratch/Flame Charge/Ember-equivalent move in earlier core sets. Do not falsely claim otherwise. No jumping while carrying/throwing the bag, no people in the landing area.

## Changes from v1

1. **Changed Blast Burn** from Medicine Ball Overhead Slam to Two-Handed Landmine Thruster. Reason: preserve and advance the forward fire-projection mechanic of Ember and Flamethrower, rather than switching to a downward floor impact. Two-handed thruster is distinct from Venusaur's unilateral landmine push press because it includes a squat-to-press with both hands.
2. **Changed Dragon Claw** from Single-Arm Low-to-High Cable Lift to Single-Arm High-to-Low Cable Chop with Controlled Step-and-Pivot. Reason: preserve the downward, claw-like diagonal established across #004 and #005, with advanced unilateral coordination rather than simply reversing direction.
3. **Kept Flare Blitz** Sled Push and **kept Seismic Toss** Sandbag Over-the-Shoulder Toss.
4. Preserved seven exercises, 22 working sets, advanced RPE and rest, two A/B video drafts, and 20-shot checklist, updated consistently with revised moves.
