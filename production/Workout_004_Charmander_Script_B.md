**Lore accuracy:** Charmander is Fire-type, and its tail flame reflects its condition. Flame Charge raises Speed; Ember has 40 base power in Scarlet/Violet; Fire Spin traps and damages its target for four to five turns; Scratch is Normal-type. “Super effective” and “my HP” are spoken jokes, not statements about real training effects. Fire Blast is a distinct Fire-type attack referenced only as a joke, **not** a fifth core move.

**Note:** The video groups the signature moves for storytelling, but the actual workout places Scratch seventh, after the supporting movements. Do not call this video order the set-by-set workout order.

**Voice notes:** Straight-faced references, not a tutorial about game mechanics. Pause slightly on “forty,” “four to five,” and “Pokémon Center.” If a joke runs long, trim pauses rather than speeding the demonstration.
