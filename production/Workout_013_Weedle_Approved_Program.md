# Workout #013 — Weedle: Sting School

**Status: Creator-approved October 27, 2026.** Exercises and matched A/B scripts published as **Review**. Filming, four actual Camera Tests and A/B results pending; not Final.

## Pokémon research and training identity

- **National Pokédex:** #013 · **Type:** Bug / Poison · **Stage:** Larva · **Counterpart:** #010 Caterpie.
- **Lore and ability:** Hairy Bug Pokémon, Bug/Poison; head stinger and silk. Shield Dust; hidden Run Away. Cousin-rival counterpart to Caterpie, favoring pointed forward attacks.
- **Relationship:** Same forest, different tactic: Caterpie pushes a light sled and braces with a sandbag; Weedle adds a controlled forward sting and walks with the same bear-hug clamp.
- **Workout goal:** Introductory precise arm drive, net-like band control, grip strength and short rope intervals.
- **Level:** Beginner+ · **Planned duration:** 40–50 minutes including setup/rest/transitions. · **Intensity:** RPE 6–7, smooth punch extension and stable balance rather than maximum speed.
- **Equipment:** Stable band anchor, two chest-height bands, a very light soft sandbag, battle ropes, dumbbells, stable incline push-up platform.
- **Evolution chain:** Weedle → Kakuna → Beedrill. **Opposing line:** Caterpie → Metapod → Butterfree.

## Complete seven-exercise workout

| Order | Move | Real exercise | Sets × dose | Rest | Key coaching cue |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Poison Sting | Standing Single-Arm Resistance-Band Punch | **3 × 8/side** | 75 sec | Stagger stance, controlled press forward, stop before elbow locks. No snap or heavy resistance. |
| 2 | String Shot | Standing Two-Arm Resistance-Band Press | **2 × 10** | 60–75 sec | Anchor the band safely behind you; press both hands forward at chest height and return slowly without flinging resistance. |
| 3 | Supporting | Reverse Lunge | **3 × 8/side** | 75–90 sec | Step back under control, balance over front leg, use rail if needed. |
| 4 | Bug Bite | Sandbag Bear-Hug Carry | **2 × 15 m** | 75 sec | Hold a very light soft sandbag securely against the ribs and walk 15 m slowly; breathe and stop before grip or posture fails. |
| 5 | Supporting | Chest-Supported Dumbbell Row | **2 × 10** | 75 sec | Row with chest supported, not with momentum. |
| 6 | Supporting | Incline Push-Up | **2 × 8–12** | 75 sec | Hands on stable bench or wall, body aligned; pick height for clean reps. |
| 7 | Electroweb | Outside-Circle Battle Rope Waves | **2 × 15 sec** | 75 sec | Make compact outward circles with space around both ropes, bend knees slightly. |

**Total:** 16 prescribed sets across seven exercises. **Signature-move sets:** 9. The sets shown are training prescriptions; additional warm-up rehearsals are not included.

### Warm-up

7–8 min: easy cardio 2 min; shoulder circles, two-arm band-press micro-reps, step-back lunge practice, 8 elevated push-ups and one 8-sec gentle outside-rope circle.

### Cooldown

3–5 min easy walking and relaxed upper-back, wrist and hip mobility.

### Regressions / progressions

- Beginner: lightest band or no band for Sting, a comfortable light band-press range, support the lunge, use an empty soft sandbag held against the ribs and walk a shorter distance, higher incline push-ups.
- Intermediate: slightly firmer band and soft sandbag, then add clean reps without chasing speed.
- Advanced: maintain fast-looking but safely controlled output and add no hard punching loads.

### Equipment and safety checks

Secure band anchors; the band is not a striking weapon. Elbows must not snap into extension. Carry a very light soft sandbag against the torso in a clear lane, wearing closed-toe shoes; set it down before the grip or posture deteriorates. Clear rope area; poison and electricity are digital only.

- Keep signature technique full-body and legible with **no special effects** on the first Camera Test; a clean filming rehearsal is possible but has not happened yet.
- Work on stable dry flooring; provide full recovery between faster or power movements. The entire session need not be completed by beginners if they require regressions.
- This is fitness-inspired entertainment, not real contact combat or a guarantee of effects from a Pokémon status move.
