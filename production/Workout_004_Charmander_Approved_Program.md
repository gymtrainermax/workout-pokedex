# Workout 004 — Charmander: First Flame
## Refined Workout Proposal v2 | October 22, 2026

**Status:** Complete workout approved and locked. Public Review / program-only release, with both A/B scripts and filming pending.

### Pokémon and training identity
- **Charmander #004 — Fire type.** Fast, animated, playful first-stage Fire starter.
- **Goal:** Short accelerations, introductory explosive power, balanced full-body strength, and controlled rotational movement.
- **Level:** Beginner+.
- **Duration:** Approximately **40–50 minutes**, depending on equipment access and transitions.
- **Strength effort:** RPE 6–7; power work ends when speed or technique deteriorates.
- **Sets:** **16**, across seven exercises, including ten signature-move sets (3+3+2+2).
- **Evolution strategy:** Keep Flamethrower open for Charmeleon and the larger Fire/Dragon techniques available for Charizard. These are creative reservations, not finalized movesets.

### Four signature move core

| Move | Type | Real exercise | Prescription | Intended Pokémon connection |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Flame Charge | Fire | Short Acceleration Run | **3 × 10–15 m**, rest **90–120 s** | Accelerate forward like a charging fire attack; Flame Charge also increases Speed in the games |
| Ember | Fire | Medicine Ball Chest Pass to Wall | **3 × 5**, rest **90 s** | Quick forward projectile representing small bursts of flame |
| Fire Spin | Fire | Landmine 180s | **2 × 8 alternating side-to-side sweeps total/set**, rest **75–90 s** | Bar traces a controlled, swirling arc analogous to a ring/vortex of fire |
| Scratch | Normal | Single-Arm High-to-Low Diagonal Cable Pulldown | **2 × 8–10 each side**, rest **75 s after both sides** | A deliberate descending and inward claw swipe; claw marks added in edit |

**Learnset:** All four appear in Charmander's Scarlet/Violet learnset (Scratch and Ember by level-up, Fire Spin by level-up or TM, Flame Charge through TM).
**Registry:** The order-insensitive four-move combination (Ember, Fire Spin, Flame Charge, Scratch) was checked against 230 entries in the Workout Dex on October 22, 2026, with no match. Recheck when publishing.

### Workout order: 16 working sets

| # | Move / exercise | Sets × reps | Rest | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Flame Charge — Short Acceleration Run** | **3 × 10–15 m** | **90–120 s** | Velocity and quickness while fresh |
| 2 | **Ember — Medicine Ball Chest Pass to Wall** | **3 × 5** | **90 s** | Upper-body explosive power while fresh |
| 3 | **Fire Spin — Landmine 180s** | **2 × 8 alternating sweeps total** | **75–90 s** | Coordinated rotational control before loaded fatigue |
| 4 | Goblet Squat | **2 × 8–10** | **90 s** | Knee-dominant leg strength absent in prior draft |
| 5 | Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift | **2 × 8–10** | **90 s** | Hip hinge and posterior-chain strength |
| 6 | Incline Push-Up | **2 × 8–12** | **75 s** | Scalable horizontal push |
| 7 | **Scratch — Single-Arm High-to-Low Diagonal Cable Pulldown** | **2 × 8–10/arm** | **75 s** | Vertical/diagonal pulling and trunk stability |

**Changes from v1 besides requested signature exercises:** Goblet Squat replaces Seated Cable Row because Scratch now supplies the primary resisted pulling pattern, and the workout otherwise lacked a knee-dominant lift. Incline Push-Up replaces Dumbbell Floor Press to separate Charmander's look from Bulbasaur and provide a scalable, athletic horizontal push. Dumbbell RDL remains to preserve the hinge. One tradeoff is **less dedicated horizontal rowing**; this is acceptable for a short athletic session, not a comprehensive all-week upper-back plan. If a dedicated horizontal row is desired, an eighth supporting exercise (2 × 10 rows) is preferable to removing the goblet squat.

### Warm-up (8–10 min)

1. 2 minutes easy cardio.
2. Ankle/hip mobility and 2 × 10–15 m increasingly brisk walks (not all-out).
3. 8 unloaded squats and 8 unloaded hip hinges.
4. Shoulder mobility and 5 elevated push-up rehearsals.
5. Two light wall-chest-pass rehearsals, several controlled unloaded landmine-arc rehearsals, and two diagonal cable pull practice reps each side.

### Cooldown (3–5 min)
Easy walking and comfortable hip, trunk, and shoulder mobility; calm breathing.

### Coaching cues and equipment notes
- **Flame Charge:** Use a clear 10–15 m lane **plus stopping space**. Start from a controlled standing or staggered stance, accelerate smoothly, decelerate progressively. Avoid maximal sprinting, slick flooring, or people crossing the lane.
- **Ember:** Choose a lightweight, designated wall-throw medicine ball and solid approved wall. Stand outside the rebound zone and retrieve safely; no unpredictable catches.
- **Fire Spin:** Use a **secured landmine anchor**, preferably a light training bar or empty bar. Soft knees, controlled arc, feet and hips may pivot. Don't wrench the lower back or allow momentum to fling the bar. The set counts **eight full side-to-side sweeps**, alternating directions, not eight on each side.
- **Goblet Squat:** Hold a manageable kettlebell/dumbbell close to the chest; feet stable, squat to an individually comfortable depth.
- **DB RDL:** Maintain a modest knee bend, hinge hips back, keep weights near legs, and stop before lumbar position changes.
- **Incline Push-Up:** Choose bench/bar height that allows a straight-line trunk and smooth shoulder movement.
- **Scratch:** Set cable pulley above shoulder height. In a stable staggered or half-kneeling stance, draw one hand diagonally down toward the opposite front pocket, arm and shoulder moving in a comfortable path, keeping trunk rotation small and controlled. Don't yank, turn it into an explosive punch, or pull the shoulder into an uncomfortable overhead position. This is a high-to-low **diagonal cable pull**, not a conventional straight-arm vertical lat pulldown.

**Equipment:** 10–15 m lane and braking area, medicine ball and approved throwing wall, properly fixed landmine bar, dumbbell/kettlebell, dumbbells, stable incline for push-ups, functional cable machine.

### Regressions and progressions

| Exercise | Beginner | Intermediate | Advanced |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flame Charge | 10 m quick walk | Smooth controlled 10–15 m acceleration | Faster controlled acceleration, preserving safe deceleration |
| Ember | Very light two-hand chest pass | Crisp light chest pass | Faster pass with safe implement |
| Fire Spin | Short-range unweighted dowel arc or light cable variation | Light secured landmine 180 | Slight increase in resistance while maintaining controlled arc |
| Goblet Squat | Bodyweight box squat | Goblet squat | Heavier goblet squat with good depth |
| DB RDL | Unloaded hinge / light DB | Moderate controlled DB RDL | Gradual resistance progression |
| Incline Push-Up | Wall push-up / higher incline | Bench incline push-up | Lower incline / floor push-up |
| Scratch | Light cable with small range | High-to-low diagonal cable pull | Moderate resistance with slow controlled return |
