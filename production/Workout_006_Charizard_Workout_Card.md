# Workout 006 | Charizard: Full Blaze

**Advanced full-body power | 65–75 min | 22 working sets**

## 4. Full seven-exercise session: 22 working sets

**Warm-up, 9–12 min (not counted as working sets):**
1. 2–3 min easy row, bike or brisk walk.
2. 8 bodyweight squats; 8 hip hinges; 6 alternating thoracic rotations/side; 8 scapular pull-ups or band pull-aparts.
3. 2 x 3 unloaded landmine squat-to-press rehearsals with a secured anchor and clear bar path.
4. Two light sandbag practice pickups/shoulderings before throwing; 2 light single-arm high-to-low cable chops/side with controlled footwork; one unloaded sled rehearsal.
5. Before work sets on front squat, 2–3 progressive warm-up sets at comfortable light-to-moderate weight. These are *not* part of the 22 working sets.

| # | Exercise and role | Sets x reps | Rest after set | Working intensity / purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Blast Burn**: Two-Handed Landmine Thruster (signature) | **3 x 5** | **90–120 sec** | Secure landmine, controlled fast squat-to-press; stop when drive slows |
| 2 | **Seismic Toss**: Sandbag Over-the-Shoulder Toss (signature) | **3 x 4 total** (2/shoulder) | **120–150 sec** | Manageable soft bag, no missed reps or uncontrolled drops |
| 3 | Barbell Front Squat (supporting) | **3 x 5** | **150–180 sec** | RPE 7–8; knee-dominant compound lower-body strength |
| 4 | **Flare Blitz**: Sled Push (signature) | **4 x 12–15 m** | **120 sec** | Moderate-heavy resistance with steady forward drive; do not grind to a halt |
| 5 | Pull-Up or Assisted Pull-Up (supporting) | **3 x 6–8** | **90–120 sec** | Controlled full range; lat and upper-back strength suggesting powerful wings, **not an official Flying move** |
| 6 | Incline Dumbbell Bench Press (supporting) | **3 x 8** | **90–120 sec** | RPE 7–8; controlled upper-body push, distinct from light Svend press |
| 7 | **Dragon Claw**: Single-Arm High-to-Low Cable Chop with Controlled Step-and-Pivot (signature) | **3 x 8/side** | **75 sec after both sides** | Light/moderate load; descending diagonal, controlled footwork and return |

**Cooldown, 3–5 min:** Easy walk, quiet nasal or natural breathing, comfortable shoulder/lat, quadriceps/hip, and thoracic mobility. No forced end-range stretching.

**Suggested time budget (approximate, includes transitions):** warm-up 9–12; landmine and sandbag power blocks 12–16; front squat and setup 10–12; sled 9–12; upper strength 12–15; cable chop 6–8; cooldown 3–5. Total roughly 65–75 minutes depending on logistics. Equipment queues can extend this estimate.

**Weekly use / load progression:** This is a demanding advanced session. Use one session initially within a balanced weekly plan. Give adequate recovery before repeating heavy lower-body or overhead work. Over 3–4 weeks, first make reps consistently crisp, then add the smallest appropriate load increase to the front squat, press or sled. Never progress the landmine power sets or sandbag tosses at the expense of safe mechanics and the landing zone. One lighter deload week may be appropriate in ongoing programming. Pain is a reason to modify or stop, not evidence that the fictional recoil effect worked.


**Official core moves:** Dragon Claw, Seismic Toss, Flare Blitz, Blast Burn.

**Evolution audit:** Scratch → Slash → Dragon Claw (descending cable path, progressively integrated); Flame Charge → Flare Blitz (unloaded sprint → loaded sled); Ember → Flamethrower → Blast Burn (forward chest action → full-body forward/upward blast). Seismic Toss adds a new aerial-grappling identity while building on DB RDL → trap-bar deadlift.
