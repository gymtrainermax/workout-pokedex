## Four filmed Camera Tests (must pass after real footage exists)

1. **Flame Charge:** Viewer recognizes *forward acceleration*, not an uncontrolled all-out sprint. Flames are digital overlays only; film a clear start and finish without a person in the lane.
2. **Ember:** Ball visibly travels forward from chest to impact; lightweight throwing ball and approved wall. Don't imply actual fire or show unsafe rebounds.
3. **Fire Spin:** Landmine arcs through a true side-to-side 180-style motion and the VFX traces that path. Verify secure base, full clearance, controlled foot/hip pivots, and no forced lumbar twisting. Eight alternating sweeps **total per set**.
4. **Scratch:** The diagonal cable handle goes downward and across in a deliberate controlled swipe, not an explosive punch or strained shoulder yank. Camera reads a single-arm slash even before claw-mark effects.

**Pokémon Test:** Four legitimate moves, Fire/Fire/Fire/Normal. **Trainer Test:** Real, purpose-fit movements as programmed, not stunt choreography. **Camera feasibility:** Promising; filmed Camera Test remains pending on all four. No in-gym ignition, sparking, flames, powder, or smoke.

## Shared visual effects

- Opening / Flame Charge: fire trail following the running lane, never obscuring the feet or braking.
- Ember: compact fireball effect follows medicine ball; wall impact only.
- Fire Spin: spiraling fire ring traces the bar's legitimate controlled arc.
- Scratch: three claw marks tracking the cable handle's descending path.
- Charmander tail: one constant, bright flame animation in both versions.
