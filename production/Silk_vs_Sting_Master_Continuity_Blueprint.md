# Silk vs. Sting — Same-name move continuity audit, v3

**Creator rule (October 27, 2026):** Identical official move names must share the same recognizable gym exercise family, irrespective of species, rivalry or evolution. The individual Pokémon may change stance, resistance, speed or volume. Creator-approved programs and matched A/B script drafts published in **Review** on October 27, 2026; filming and all Camera Tests pending.

## Existing published move baseline

- **Tackle** — Bulbasaur: Sled Push; Squirtle: Light Sled March. Both belong to the forward *sled-propulsion* family. The proposed Caterpie and Metapod workouts were amended to match this family.
- No already-published workout #001–009 is edited by these proposals.

## Evolution/rivalry arcs

**Caterpie → Metapod → Butterfree:** A low-stance sled push becomes a slower paused sled push during the cocoon stage. Single-arm forward band silk becomes half-kneeling paused cable silk. Sandbag clamping stays recognizably the same. After metamorphosis, Butterfree expresses wings and control via Quiver Dance, Air Slash and calm Sleep Powder, and Caterpie's rope ripples scale into Butterfree's **Bug Buzz**. New move names are *thematic* progressions, not alleged canon move evolution.

**Weedle → Kakuna → Beedrill:** Weedle's Poison Sting band punch freezes as Kakuna's held band punch; String Shot's two-arm forward band press acquires a pause; a sandbag bear-hug carry freezes into a sandbag bear-hug hold. Beedrill develops quick cable Twineedle strikes, loaded landmine Poison Jab and fast sled Fell Stinger. No real stingers or human targets.

**Paired rivals:** Larvae share forward web presses, sandbag clamps and two-rope web effects. Cocoons both use forearm planks but with distinct bracing emphasis. Finals diverge Butterfree special/status control vs. Beedrill physical/poison attacks. The original anime's Sleep Powder versus Twineedle creates a thematic final battle, without portraying harm.

## Quality and filming rules

- For any repeated move name, compare clean effects-free 3/4 and side angles. Ensure the same apparatus, forward drive/brace/clamp direction and silhouette reads before digital effects.
- Keep each program at exactly four legitimate moves, seven exercises, same total prescribed sets (16,15,20,16,15,21 for National #010–015; Butterfree's total includes two breathing rounds rather than all strength working sets), all filmed Camera Tests pending.
- Each species keeps two matched 70-second A/B drafts with identical timing, footage and on-screen prescription, changing only dialogue and dialogue subtitles. Final video quality is not approved without filming.
- Workouts #010–015 have creator-approved exercise programming and A/B scripts, published as **Review**. None is filmed or Final. Prior published #001–009 remain unchanged.
