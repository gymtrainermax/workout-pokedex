# Workouts 007–009: The Water Starter Evolution Plan
**Creator-approved water starter trilogy • Published for Review October 27, 2026.** Workouts and moves locked; filming and four Camera Tests per stage pending.

## Four movement families that evolve together

| Training family | Squirtle #007 | Wartortle #008 | Blastoise #009 | What the viewer sees |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Water projection** | **Water Gun**: Standing Single-Arm Resistance-Band Press | **Water Pulse**: Half-Kneeling Single-Arm Cable Press | **Hydro Cannon**: Standing Dual-Cable Power Press | Progressive resistance + stability + two-arm cannon projection. Squirtle band → Wartortle cable anti-rotation → Blastoise dual-cable speed and recovery. |
| **Shell defense** | **Withdraw**: Forearm Plank | **Protect**: Tall-Kneeling Pallof Press | **Iron Defense**: Double-Kettlebell Front-Rack Carry | Isometric trunk brace → resist lateral pull → carry external weight without losing upright posture. |
| **Rotational water power** | **Rapid Spin**: Standing Resistance-Band Trunk Rotation | **Aqua Tail**: Landmine 180s | **Surf**: Rotational Medicine Ball Scoop Toss to Wall | Start controlled trunk+hip turn → create bigger weighted sweep → safely transfer rotation into a powerful throw/wave. |
| **Water-powered charge** | **Tackle**: Light Sled March | **Aqua Jet**: Light Sled Push (Acceleration Focus) | **Wave Crash**: Moderately Heavy Sled Push | Deliberate march → faster acceleration → stronger loaded drive. Wave Crash recoil appears only in jokes/VFX. |

| Episode | Stage | Work sets | Duration | Learning objective |

## Editorial research and legality
- All three species are Water-type. Squirtle evolves into Wartortle at level 16, then into Blastoise at level 36.
- All twelve proposed moves can legitimately be obtained in Scarlet/Violet. Wartortle Aqua Jet is retained from a Squirtle egg move, not a Wartortle level-up move.
- Blastoise Hydro Cannon is TM154 in Scarlet/Violet and is the Water-starter ultimate move parallel to Venusaur Frenzy Plant and Charizard Blast Burn.
- Move types by stage: Squirtle [Water, Normal, Normal, Water]; Wartortle [Water, Water, Water, Normal]; Blastoise [Water, Water, Water, Steel]. The website has all 18 icons, so no new type graphics should be required.
- The three unordered four-move cores are different from each other and from already published #001–#006.
