# WORKOUT 007 | Squirtle: Shell School
**Status:** Seven-exercise program and four moves creator-approved October 27, 2026, published as **Review**. A/B scripts are production drafts; not filmed; all four filmed Camera Tests remain pending.
**Level:** Beginner+ · **Duration:** approximately 40–50 min · **Working sets:** 16 · **Type:** Water.

## Pokémon research and training identity
Tiny Turtle Pokémon, Water type. Accurate water spray, curved defensive shell and compact body. The Squirtle Squad is an anime joke, not a source for learnset legality.
Friendly, quick and defensive. Learn four basic movement skills without heavy loading.
**Intensity:** RPE 6–7 on resistance exercises; sled and band work should remain crisp, never maximal.
**Equipment:** Light loadable sled, long resistance band and secure anchor, dumbbells, goblet weight, chest-supported bench, exercise mat.

## Official four-move signature core

| Pokémon move | Type | Real exercise | Dose | Story / biomechanics |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Water Gun** | Water | Standing Single-Arm Resistance-Band Press | 3 × 10/side; rest 60–75 sec | Start from one hand at chest level and press straight forward into controlled band resistance. The first small, accurate water jet. |
| **Tackle** | Normal | Light Sled March | 3 × 12 m; rest 75–90 sec | Walk forward in a braced lean, driving each step through the floor. Controlled forward propulsion, not a sprint. |
| **Rapid Spin** | Normal | Standing Resistance-Band Trunk Rotation | 2 × 8/side; rest 60–75 sec | Pivot feet and hips together to sweep the arms across the body. The shell turns as one unit; avoid twisting only through the lower back. |
| **Withdraw** | Water | Forearm Plank | 2 × 25–35 sec; rest 45–60 sec | Ribs over pelvis, gentle glute tension, steady breathing. The basic shell-bracing skill, not a challenge to hold the longest. |

## Complete seven-exercise workout
**Warm-up:** 7–8 minutes: 2 minutes brisk walking or cycling; 8 air squats, 8 unloaded hinges, shoulder circles; easy standing band presses, low-tension trunk rotations and one very light sled pass. Warm-up sets are not working sets.

| Order | Signature move | Exercise | Work | Rest | Technique / intent |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tackle | **Light Sled March** | 3 × 12 m | 75–90 sec | Light sled, continuous deliberate steps, shoulders away from ears. |
| 2 | Water Gun | **Standing Single-Arm Resistance-Band Press** | 3 × 10/side | 60–75 sec | Secure band anchor, smooth return, keep ribs stacked. |
| 3 | Supporting | **Goblet Squat** | 2 × 8–10 | 75–90 sec | Stand tall, keep whole foot grounded, stop at comfortable depth. |
| 4 | Rapid Spin | **Standing Resistance-Band Trunk Rotation** | 2 × 8/side | 60–75 sec | Rotate hips and feet as a unit, light band tension only. |
| 5 | Supporting | **Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift** | 2 × 8–10 | 75–90 sec | Soft knees, move hips backward, flat backed controllable range. |
| 6 | Supporting | **Chest-Supported Dumbbell Row** | 2 × 10 | 75 sec | Keep chest supported, row elbows toward back pockets. |
| 7 | Withdraw | **Forearm Plank** | 2 × 25–35 sec | 45–60 sec | Brace comfortably while breathing; stop if hips sag. |

**Cooldown:** 3–5 minutes easy walking and relaxed shoulder, hip and thoracic mobility. No forced stretching.

## Level modifications
- **Beginner:** Shorter 10 m sled march; lighter band; supported box squat; empty-hand hip hinge; light single-arm supported row; incline forearm plank or shorter 15–20 sec holds.
- **Intermediate:** Increase band tension modestly, use a slightly heavier light sled, goblet squat and dumbbells at RPE 7, extend plank only while breathing is calm.
- **Advanced:** Repeat as a technique-focused day; do not turn a beginner episode into a maximal sled or plank test.

## Safety and load progression
Anchor resistance bands to a rated fixed point, never an unstable rack attachment. Verify sled lane is clear and no one walks into the path. Use foot pivot for rotations, not ballistic lower-back twisting; keep plank lengths comfortable.
Log the exercise, load, distance and technique quality. Progress one variable at a time across sessions; do not force all three evolutionary workouts into a single day. Power sets end when speed or form decreases. Stop for pain or dizziness.
