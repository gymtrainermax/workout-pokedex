# WORKOUT 001 — BULBASAUR
## Approved training program + video concept, version 1.0

**Series:** The Original 151 / Workout Pokédex
**Identity:** Seedling Strength / Beginner+ / full-body strength, controlled power, short conditioning intervals
**Workout approval:** Programming approved by creator on October 21, 2026. Filming, on-camera move tests, social package, and complete Series Standards v1.1 final audit are **pending**. Do not mark the entry Final or describe its video as completed.
**Duration:** About 40–50 minutes inclusive of warm-up and cooldown (actual time depends on equipment availability).
**Working intensity:** Around RPE 6–7 for the resistance exercises; explosive work is intentionally light and crisp; no sets to failure.
**Working volume:** 15–16 work sets/intervals.

### Four signature Pokémon moves (approved exercise selections)

1. **Seed Bomb** — Medicine Ball Slam. Official move confirmed in later-generation Bulbasaur learnsets. A planted/smashed seed, with impact effects for video. The ball is a **lightweight non-bouncing slam ball**. Avoid uncontrolled overhead arching or rebound-catching. **2 × 6; 90 sec rest.**
2. **Tackle** — Sled Push. Forward drive matches a tackle. Begin with light resistance and a clear straight lane. **3 × 12–15 m; 90 sec rest.**
3. **Razor Leaf** — High-to-Low Dual-Cable X Crossover. Hold a high handle in each hand, pull diagonally downward/inward, and gently cross the hands into an X at a comfortable distance in front of the body. Not a twisting woodchop; avoid forced shoulder range or cable rubbing. Light resistance. **2 × 8 total; 60–75 sec rest.**
4. **Vine Whip** — Alternating Battle Rope Waves. Alternating whips visually resemble vines. **2–3 × 15 seconds; 60–75 sec rest.**

**Signature presentation order in promo:** Seed Bomb, Tackle, Razor Leaf, Vine Whip (matched to approved session order). The uniqueness registry currently contains no conflicting recorded core combination; maintain that check as future Pokémon workouts are approved.

### Approved complete session — exercise order is locked

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | Main cue |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Seed Bomb — Medicine Ball Slam | 2 × 6 | 90 s | Drive down sharply without leaning backward; dead ball; controlled pickup. |
| 2 | Tackle — Sled Push | 3 × 12–15 m | 90 s | Brace, lean as needed, drive through full foot; clear lane. |
| 3 | Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift | 2 × 8–10 | 90 s | Soft knees, hips back, weights close, controlled lower. |
| 4 | Dumbbell Floor Press | 2 × 8–10 | 75 s | Stable upper arms, wrists stacked, gentle floor contact. |
| 5 | Seated Cable Row | 2 × 10 | 75 s | Stable torso, pull elbows back without momentum. |
| 6 | Razor Leaf — High-to-Low Dual-Cable X Crossover | 2 × 8 total | 60–75 s | Soft elbows, light cables, comfortable X, don't force hand crossing. |
| 7 | Vine Whip — Alternating Battle Rope Waves | 2–3 × 15 s | 60–75 s | Athletic base, alternate arms, stop when wave control declines. |

**Warm-up (7–8 min):** 2–3 min easy cardio, bodyweight squats, unloaded hip hinges, gentle shoulder movement, then light medicine-ball and sled rehearsals.

**Cooldown (3–5 min):** Relaxed walk, normal breathing, comfortable chest/shoulder/hip movement.

**Regressions:** Very light slam ball with reduced overhead excursion or slam pattern without release; unloaded sled / short push; light dumbbells or dowel hinge; light floor press; resistance-band row; light/short-range cable X; 10–12 second low-amplitude ropes.
**Progressions:** Increase sled resistance or distance in small increments; progress RDL and press load gradually; increase rope rounds only if waves stay crisp; do not increase X-crossover load at expense of shoulder comfort.
**Safety and equipment:** Use a purpose-built non-bouncing slam ball in a permitted lane; never catch a high-bounce medicine ball on rebound; footwear with traction; secure dual cable handles, keep wrists neutral, stay within comfortable range; don't let the cable handles collide. If the gym lacks suitable equipment, adaptations require renewed programming approval rather than silently replacing the signature exercises.

### Pokémon authenticity notes

- Bulbasaur (#001) is a Grass/Poison Seed Pokémon with a seed/bulb on its back and vine-like appendages.
- Tackle, Vine Whip, Razor Leaf, and Seed Bomb are official Bulbasaur moves in Scarlet/Violet and other later games. Seed Bomb was introduced in Generation IV, not present in Red/Blue.
- Sources: https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Bulbasaur_%28Pok%C3%A9mon%29/Generation_IX_learnset ; https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Seed_Bomb
