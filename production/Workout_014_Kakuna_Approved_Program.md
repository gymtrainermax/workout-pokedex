# Workout #014 — Kakuna: Sting Guard

**Status: Creator-approved October 27, 2026.** Exercises and matched A/B scripts published as **Review**. Filming, four actual Camera Tests and A/B results pending; not Final.

## Pokémon research and training identity

- **National Pokédex:** #014 · **Type:** Bug / Poison · **Stage:** Cocoon · **Counterpart:** #011 Metapod.
- **Lore and ability:** Cocoon Pokémon, Bug/Poison type; Shed Skin. Stays encased while Beedrill’s aggressive physical attacks develop; more upright and pointed defense than Metapod.
- **Relationship:** Kakuna and Metapod share Harden but show opposing expressions: both use forearm planks, with Kakuna gently pressing its forearms into the floor.
- **Workout goal:** Deliberate plank bracing, restrained stinger extension, net tension and grip control before Beedrill emerges.
- **Level:** Beginner+ / Recovery-Strength · **Planned duration:** 35–45 minutes including setup/rest/transitions. · **Intensity:** RPE 5–6 on most work, deliberately low-velocity. Short quality holds beat maximal strain.
- **Equipment:** Comfortable exercise mat, secure resistance bands, one light soft sandbag, goblet load, chest-supported bench, stable support for lateral lunge.
- **Evolution chain:** Weedle → Kakuna → Beedrill. **Opposing line:** Caterpie → Metapod → Butterfree.

## Complete seven-exercise workout

| Order | Move | Real exercise | Sets × dose | Rest | Key coaching cue |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Harden | Forearm Plank with Active Floor Push | **3 × 20 sec** | 90 sec | On a comfortable mat, keep elbows under shoulders and gently press forearms into the floor; maintain calm breathing and a neutral back. |
| 2 | String Shot | Standing Two-Arm Resistance-Band Press with Pause | **2 × 8, 2-sec pause** | 75 sec | Anchor bands behind at chest height; press both hands forward, hold for 2 seconds and control the return. |
| 3 | Supporting | Goblet Squat | **2 × 8** | 90 sec | Moderate light load, upright torso, stable depth. |
| 4 | Poison Sting | Split-Stance Single-Arm Band Punch Isometric | **2 × 15 sec/side** | 75 sec | Hold light band at forward extension without locked elbow, trunk steady, reset slowly. |
| 5 | Supporting | Chest-Supported Dumbbell Row | **2 × 10** | 75 sec | Full support and slow eccentrics. |
| 6 | Bug Bite | Sandbag Bear-Hug Hold | **2 × 25 sec** | 75–90 sec | Hold the same very light soft sandbag against the torso, stand tall and breathe; stop before grip slips. |
| 7 | Supporting | Supported Lateral Lunge | **2 × 6/side** | 75 sec | Hold upright support as needed and shift hips sideways without knee collapse. |

**Total:** 15 prescribed sets across seven exercises. **Signature-move sets:** 9. The sets shown are training prescriptions; additional warm-up rehearsals are not included.

### Warm-up

6–8 min gentle cycling or walking, shoulder blade glides, supported squats and lunges, easy band press and easy forearm-plank and light sandbag hold rehearsals.

### Cooldown

3–5 min easy breathing and comfortable shoulder/wrist/hip movement.

### Regressions / progressions

- Beginner: an inclined forearm plank against a stable bench or wall, nearly slack band and an empty soft sandbag hug, supported lunges and wall rows.
- Intermediate: perform all 15 sets with steady tension and unhurried movement.
- Advanced: use the session for bracing technique and recovery, then save high-speed stinging for Beedrill.

### Equipment and safety checks

Do not brace to exhaustion or hold the breath during planks; place forearms comfortably, secure band anchors, and handle light sandbags in a clear space. Poison effects are digital only.

- Keep signature technique full-body and legible with **no special effects** on the first Camera Test; a clean filming rehearsal is possible but has not happened yet.
- Work on stable dry flooring; provide full recovery between faster or power movements. The entire session need not be completed by beginners if they require regressions.
- This is fitness-inspired entertainment, not real contact combat or a guarantee of effects from a Pokémon status move.
