## Filming order: group by gym equipment, not edit order

Make a separate **clean technique take** (usable for instructional clips) and a **performance take** (usable for the Reel). Shoot at **vertical 9:16**, 30 fps baseline, higher frame rate where useful for crisp motion, with safe space around the person and equipment. Keep 9:16 overlays inside platform-safe regions, especially the lower screen.

1. **A-roll / speaking**: camera-facing hook; “Looks pretty harmless, right?”; post-ropes reaction; two alternate endings. Chest-up tripod framing; leave side negative space for Bulbasaur overlay.
2. **Seed Bomb / slam ball**: locked three-quarter full-body frame, ball and floor contact visible; then separate low impact close-up from a safe offset angle; never place camera/operator in ball landing zone.
3. **Tackle / sled push**: low front three-quarter with unobstructed lane, plus side view of braced torso, feet, and sled. Keep sled path completely clear.

5. **Vine Whip / alternating ropes**: front three-quarter wide showing both wave paths and full rope anchor direction; second detail shot of hands and waves, avoiding blocked faces.
6. **Three supporting lifts**: one clean rep each of dumbbell RDL, dumbbell floor press, and seated cable row, with identifiable equipment.
7. **B-roll**: #001 card, four Pokémon move-title inserts, hands setting cable heights, putting down slam ball, sled side profile, sweat/water/reaction, cover-frame pose.

## Four Camera Tests: pass/fail criteria

- **Seed Bomb / Medicine Ball Slam:** Does it genuinely look like a seed striking a target? Whole slam and impact clear, no bounce, no over-arched back. If not, adjust angle/effect, do not change locked exercise without review.
- **Tackle / Sled Push:** Does a viewer see forward driving force instantly? Feet, torso, sled motion and clear path in shot.
- **Razor Leaf / Dual-Cable X Crossover:** Do two clear downward sweeps meet in a distinct X? High pulleys in frame, wrists/cables comfortable, no equipment collision. This is the highest-priority camera test.
- **Vine Whip / Alternating Battle Rope Waves:** Do two alternating whipping waves read clearly on camera? Both ropes visible with rhythm and background contrast.

## Approved workout, unchanged

**Beginner+; approximately 40–50 minutes; strength RPE ~6–7; 7–8 minute warm-up and 3–5 minute cooldown.**

| Workout order | Pokémon/fitness exercise | Prescribed dose | Rest |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Seed Bomb** — Medicine Ball Slam | **2 × 6** | 90 s |
| 2 | **Tackle** — Sled Push | **3 × 12–15 m** | 90 s |
| 3 | Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift | **2 × 8–10** | 90 s |
| 4 | Dumbbell Floor Press | **2 × 8–10** | 75 s |
| 5 | Seated Cable Row | **2 × 10** | 75 s |
| 6 | **Razor Leaf** — High-to-Low Dual-Cable X Crossover | **2 × 8 total** | 60–75 s |
| 7 | **Vine Whip** — Alternating Battle Rope Waves | **2–3 × 15 s** | 60–75 s |

Warm up: 2–3 minutes light cardio, bodyweight squats, unloaded hinges, shoulder mobility, then light ball/sled rehearsals. Cool down with easy walking and comfortable shoulder/hip mobility. Use a **light, non-bouncing slam ball** in a permitted area and avoid uncontrolled catches; empty/light sled to start; use **light, controlled cable loads** with natural shoulders and wrists; stop rope sets as coordination fades. Support exercise regressions and progressions remain as specified in the approved v1 program.
