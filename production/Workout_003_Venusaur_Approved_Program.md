# Workout 003 — Venusaur: Solar Overload

**Status:** WORKOUT PROGRAM APPROVED AND LOCKED on 2026-10-22; publicly released in Review (not Final). A/B scripts and filmed Camera Tests remain pending.

**Pokémon:** #003 Venusaur • Grass/Poison • final evolution of Bulbasaur.
**Workout identity:** Advanced-intermediate; full-body strength + explosive power, with a balanced push/pull and squat/hinge foundation.
**Time:** Approximately 55–70 minutes including warm-up, ramp-up sets, transitions and cooldown; longer in a busy gym.
**Effort:** Main lifts RPE 7–8; cable row, bench and RDL around RPE 7; explosive work fast and clean at moderate effort; accessory pull-aparts RPE 6–7.

## Four approved signature moves

| Official move | Type | Actual exercise | Why it fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Solar Beam** | Grass | Standing Single-Arm Landmine Push Press | Deliberate preparation followed by a quick forward-and-upward launch. Overlay a solar beam along the bar path; the charge is a film beat, not a strenuous hold. |
| **Frenzy Plant** | Grass | Double-Arm Battle Rope Power Slams | Both ropes strike down forcefully like roots slamming into the ground. A more powerful variation of Bulbasaur's alternating Vine Whip waves. |
| **Giga Drain** | Grass | **Half-Kneeling Single-Arm Cable Row** | The pull draws resistance toward the body. Optional VFX show green energy traveling from a target to the trainer; no real energy restoration is claimed. |
| **Poison Powder** | Poison | **Resistance Band Pull-Apart** | Opening both arms mimics Venusaur spreading its flower and spores. Add purple particles in editing only; never use actual powder or aerosol. |

## Approved full workout in exercise order

### Warm-up: 8–10 minutes

3–4 minutes of easy cycling or walking; 5–8 controlled bodyweight squats; 6–8 unloaded hip hinges; a few shoulder circles and light band pull-aparts; then low-load landmine presses and low-intensity two-arm rope slams to practice the two power patterns. Before the squat working sets, do 2–4 progressively heavier **ramp-up sets** with a comfortable rep count; these are not counted in the 18 working sets.

| Order | Pokémon move / exercise | Sets and reps | Rest | Intensity and priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Solar Beam — Standing Single-Arm Landmine Push Press** | **3 × 5/side** | **120 seconds after both sides** | Light to moderate, fast press; stop before speed degrades. |
| **2** | **Frenzy Plant — Double-Arm Battle Rope Power Slams** | **3 × 10 seconds** | **90 seconds** | Crisp, coordinated rope slams; not an all-out conditioning bout. |
| **3** | **Barbell Back Squat** | **3 × 5–6** | **150–180 seconds** | Main strength lift, RPE ~7–8, with safeties or spotter. |
| **4** | **Giga Drain — Half-Kneeling Single-Arm Cable Row** | **3 × 8–10/side** | **75–90 seconds after both sides** | RPE ~7; controlled pull and return with square hips and ribs. |
| **5** | **Flat Dumbbell Bench Press** | **2 × 8–10** | **90 seconds** | RPE ~7; use comfortable shoulder range. |
| **6** | **Barbell Romanian Deadlift** | **2 × 8** | **90–120 seconds** | RPE ~7; hip hinge, keep bar close. |
| **7** | **Poison Powder — Resistance Band Pull-Apart** | **2 × 12–15** | **60 seconds** | Light-to-moderate band, soft elbows, open arms without shrugging or hyperextending the low back. |

**Working-set total:** 18 (3+3+3+3+2+2+2). Each unilateral set includes both sides; do not count each arm as an additional full working set. Note that unilateral work adds time even though the set count is unchanged.

### Cooldown: 3–5 minutes

Comfortable walking, a few easy shoulder movements, and slow natural breathing. No aggressive stretching immediately after heavy lifting required.

## Why these refinements make the full workout better

1. **Solar Beam moves to first:** The landmine push press is a high-skill power exercise; priority before any rope fatigue gives it a better technical and camera result.
2. **Frenzy Plant stays brief:** Ten-second rope sets preserve speed and visual impact without undermining subsequent squat strength.
3. **Giga Drain becomes a unilateral horizontal pull:** Cable rowing is a legitimate major upper-back exercise and also challenges trunk control in half kneeling. A neutral-grip pulldown is no longer necessary. The full session still includes a horizontal push (DB bench), leg strength (back squat), and hinge (RDL).
4. **Poison Powder becomes a shoulder-friendly accessory:** A light band pull-apart is a clear outward-spreading gesture, a simple filming setup, and a better fit as the final move than adding another strenuous press.
5. **Time allowance grows modestly:** Working unilateral rows on both sides can be slower than bilateral pulldowns, so 55–70 minutes is more realistic than guaranteeing 55–65.

## Coaching and adaptations

| Movement | Main cue / safety check | Beginner regression | Advanced progression |
|---|---|---|---|
| Solar Beam | Small knee dip, drive through legs, press up and forward, keep ribs stacked and torso square | Two-hand landmine press without knee drive | Slightly greater resistance while maintaining speed |
| Frenzy Plant | Brace, hinge gently, send ropes down together, don't whip into other people | Lighter ropes or controlled 6–8 sec waves | Slightly higher force without lengthening past good technique |
| Barbell Back Squat | Stable stance, appropriate depth, progressive warm-up and safety pins | Goblet squat or box squat | Add load gradually at same target RPE |
| Giga Drain | Kneel on pad, cable around lower-rib/chest height, pull elbow toward ribs, don't twist or rock backward | Seated cable row or lighter half-kneeling row | More cable resistance or 1–2 sec controlled return |
| DB Bench Press | Stable feet and shoulder blades, avoid painful ranges | Lighter DB floor press | Increase DB load within target rep range |
| Barbell RDL | Hinge, slight knee softness, bar close, stop when hip motion ends | Dumbbell RDL with reduced range | More load with a clean hinge |
| Poison Powder | Light band, arms at chest height, soft elbows, shoulder blades move smoothly, no shrugging | Thinner band or wider hand spacing | Slightly stronger band without sacrificing range |

**General modifications:** The advanced-intermediate label is an episode theme, not a prerequisite; choose a sensible weight and skip or regress any movement that causes pain. A trainer familiar with these lifts can help if someone is unsure of technique.
