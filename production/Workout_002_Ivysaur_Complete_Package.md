# Workout 002: Ivysaur — Rooted Power

**Status:** Seven-exercise workout approved on October 21, 2026; videos and camera tests pending.  
**Identity:** Intermediate, strength + power + stability; about 45–55 min.  
**Production concept:** “So You're Training Ivysaur?” Viewer is the trainer; GymTrainerMax addresses them directly. Professor Oak appears only in Workout 000.  
**A/B principle:** Shared camera footage and editing, two dialogue tracks and corresponding subtitle tracks.  
**Target:** ~68 seconds, 9:16 vertical.  

### Canonical four-move core

| Official move | Type | Fitness mapping | Training prescription |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sludge Bomb | Poison | Rotational Medicine Ball Wall Throw | 2 × 5/side, 90-sec rest |
| Growth | Normal | Dumbbell Thruster | 3 × 6–8, 90–120-sec rest |
| Grassy Glide | Grass | Lateral Slider Lunge | 2 × 8/side, 75-sec rest |
| Synthesis | Grass | 90/90 Diaphragmatic Breathing | 2 × 5 slow breaths, comfortable pace |

Source check: Ivysaur learns all four in *Scarlet/Violet* (Growth and Synthesis via level-up; Sludge Bomb TM148; Grassy Glide TM194). Grassy Glide gets priority only with Grassy Terrain. Synthesis restores Pokémon HP in-game; breathwork is a practical recovery drill, not literal healing. Reference: https://pokemondb.net/pokedex/ivysaur/moves/9 and https://pokemondb.net/move/synthesis

### Approved full workout (unchanged)

**Warm-up 7–8 minutes:** Light cardio, unloaded squats/hinges, shoulder preparation, lateral steps, and light rotational throw rehearsals.

| Order | Exercise | Sets / reps | Rest |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Sludge Bomb** — Rotational Medicine Ball Wall Throw | 2 × 5/side | 90 sec |
| 2 | Trap Bar Deadlift | 3 × 6 | 120 sec |
| 3 | **Growth** — Dumbbell Thruster | 3 × 6–8 | 90–120 sec |
| 4 | Incline Dumbbell Bench Press | 2 × 8–10 | 75–90 sec |
| 5 | Chest-Supported Dumbbell Row | 3 × 10 | 75–90 sec |
| 6 | **Grassy Glide** — Lateral Slider Lunge | 2 × 8/side | 75 sec |
| 7 | **Synthesis** — 90/90 Diaphragmatic Breathing | 2 × 5 breaths | Comfortable pace |

**Cooldown 3–5 min total:** Synthesis starts the cooldown; then easy walking / gentle mobility. Main strength sets approximately RPE 7–8, thrusters and slides 6–7; throws crisp and submaximal. Avoid invented medical promises about Synthesis.

## Safety and scaling notes

- Rotational wall throw: only where throws are permitted; use appropriate medicine ball and impact wall, no loose equipment/bystanders, no unpredictable catch or rebound. Start lighter than you think you need. Avoid forcing lumbar twisting; pivot through feet and hips.
- Trap-bar deadlift: start with high handles or lighter load for regression, brace, stay controlled, keep bar path centered. Progress load only when quality holds.
- Thruster: choose dumbbells that allow a clean overhead path and comfortable shoulder range. Light dumbbells or squat-to-reach for regression.
- Incline press and supported row: appropriate weights, comfortable range, no jerking.
- Slider lunge: short-range supported version for beginners; don't chase range by collapsing inward at knee; long-range/harder slider for advanced.
- Synthesis: 90/90 diaphragmatic breathing should be calm and natural, with no extended breath holds or exaggerated respiratory claims. Breathing is a cooldown aid, not a substitute for recovery time.
- RPE ~7–8 main strength work; ~6–7 on thrusters/lunges; power throws crisp rather than max-effort. The seven-exercise workout remains unchanged.

## A/B test design

**Hypothesis:** B's genuine Pokémon-game terminology increases completion and sharing among fans; A's accessible humor may work better across general fitness viewers.

1. Use the same platform, similar posting conditions, same cover, CTA, captions, hashtags, duration, music, and footage for both scripts.
2. Best-case: platform-supported randomized audience split. Otherwise, comparable time slots are an *observational* comparison, not a true controlled experiment.
3. Measure 3-second hold %, average view duration, completion %, saves per 1,000 views, shares per 1,000 views, and Workout Dex link visits per 1,000 views if available.
4. Track date/time, algorithmic reach and audience composition; avoid declaring a winner from small samples or unmatched distribution.
5. Ask qualitatively whether viewers understood the *real workout* (exercise names, sets/reps, safety) in both.
