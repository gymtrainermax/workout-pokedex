# Route One Rodents: Rattata → Raticate
**Workouts #019–020 | Connected Kanto Normal-type two-stage evolution | DRAFT, creator approval pending**

## Species and selection
Rattata is the Mouse Pokémon, Normal type. It evolves at **level 20** into Kanto Raticate, also Normal type. Alolan Rattata and Raticate are separate Dark/Normal forms and are not used in this arc. Learnsets are Generation VII Ultra Sun and Ultra Moon (not Scarlet/Violet). **Raticate Iron Tail is from a move tutor.**

## Four paired training threads
| Thread | Rattata #019 | Raticate #020 | Same exercise DNA |
|---|---|---|---|
| Forward drive / Tackle | Light Sled Push, 3 × 10 m | Moderately Loaded Sled Push, 4 × 12 m | Exact same official move, same sled family, progressed load and distance |
| Jaw-clamp grip | Bite: Rubber-Coated Plate Pinch Hold, 2 × 20 sec | Hyper Fang: Rubber-Coated Plate Pinch Carry, 3 × 15 m | Controlled plate clamp progresses from static to moving load |
| Tail control | Tail Whip: single-arm hip-level band sweep | Iron Tail: single-arm hip-level cable sweep with pivot | Same sweep height and hand path with heavier external resistance |
| First response | Quick Attack: short split-stance acceleration | Sucker Punch: split-stance cable punch on cue | Both start with alert, controlled initiation; different move names so evolution is *thematic*, not literal |

## Cross-Pokédex same-name continuity
- **Tackle:** Bulbasaur Sled Push; Squirtle Light Sled March; Caterpie Low-Stance Light Sled Push; Metapod Paused Light Sled Push; Pidgey Light Sled Push. Rattata and Raticate both stay on the forward sled-push family.
- **Quick Attack:** Pidgey's Short Acceleration Run from Split Stance = Rattata's Short Acceleration Run from Split Stance. Slightly different distance/intent is acceptable; not a different exercise.
- **Bite and Bug Bite are different official names.** They may share gripping themes without being exact same-name moves.

## Distinct programming
Rattata is Beginner+, **16 working sets**, 40–50 minutes, practical speed control and entry-level bracing. Raticate is Intermediate+, **22 working sets**, 55–65 minutes, more pronounced full-body strength and loaded carries. These are different alternative workouts, not a combined super-session.
