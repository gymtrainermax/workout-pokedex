# Flight School: Pidgey → Pidgeotto → Pidgeot
**Workouts #016–018 | Connected three-stage training design | Creator-approved for Review October 27, 2026; filming and Camera Tests pending**

## Official identity
- **#016 Pidgey:** Normal/Flying, Tiny Bird Pokémon. Protective sand and quick short movements.
- **#017 Pidgeotto:** Normal/Flying, larger territorial bird, sharper vision and stronger predatory wings.
- **#018 Pidgeot:** Normal/Flying, powerful wings and high-speed flight in Pokédex descriptions. This is standard Pidgeot, not Mega Pidgeot.
- Pidgey evolves at **level 18**; Pidgeotto evolves at **level 36**.
- Selected learnset reference: **Ultra Sun / Ultra Moon (Generation VII)**. Later games differ. Pidgeot Brave Bird is an **egg move inherited from a bred Pidgey**, not an ordinary level-up move.

## Four linked training themes
| Theme | Pidgey #016 | Pidgeotto #017 | Pidgeot #018 | Same movement DNA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wind | **Gust**: Alternating Battle Rope Waves | **Whirlwind**: Outside-Circle Battle Rope Waves | **Hurricane**: Double-Arm Outside-Circle Battle Rope Power Waves | Same anchored rope station; larger/stronger waves each stage |
| Wing / accuracy | **Sand Attack**: Single-Arm Low-to-High Band Sweep | **Wing Attack**: Standing Single-Arm High-to-Low Cable Sweep | **Air Slash**: Half-Kneeling Single-Arm High-to-Low Cable Sweep | Diagonal arm path becomes more controlled and loaded |
| Speed | **Quick Attack**: Short Acceleration Run | **Agility**: Lateral Shuffle into Acceleration | **Tailwind**: Mini-Band Lateral Shuffle | Small takeoff → lateral maneuvering → resisted footwork |
| Forward force | **Tackle**: Light Sled Push | **Trap Bar Deadlift**: Supporting strength bridge | **Brave Bird**: Moderately Heavy Sled Push | Sled push returns with greater intent and load; deadlift is NOT an official precursor move |

**Important distinction:** These are *creative exercise-progressions*, not claims that one move mechanically transforms into another in the games. Whirlwind is a Normal-type status move; Agility is a Psychic-type status move; Tailwind boosts allied Speed; Roost is not a healing exercise.

## Workloads, caution and equipment
| Episode | Identity | Volume | Duration | Emphasis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| #016 Pidgey | Beginner+ | **16 working sets** | 40–50 min | Safe launch, simple ropes, light sled, band sweep |
| #017 Pidgeotto | Intermediate | **16 strength/athletic sets + 2 balance holds** | 50–60 min | More coordination and control, no maximal lateral cuts |
| #018 Pidgeot | Advanced | **22 working sets** | 65–75 min | Greater force with sufficient recovery and crisp deceleration |

Individual workouts are alternatives at three different stages, **not** a prescription to perform all three in one day. Choose RPE appropriate to training experience and preserve rest periods.

## Prior same-name move audit
- **Tackle:** Previously Bulbasaur Sled Push, Squirtle Light Sled March, Caterpie Low-Stance Light Sled Push, Metapod Paused Light Sled Push. Pidgey Light Sled Push fits the exact same functional and visual family.
- **Air Slash:** Butterfree Half-Kneeling Single-Arm High-to-Low Cable Sweep. Pidgeot must retain **this same gym exercise** with modest stance/load changes only, and the final draft uses the identical exercise name.
- No four-move combination duplicates another among the 15 published Pokémon or this trio.
