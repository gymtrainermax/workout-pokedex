# Workout #022 — Fearow: Aerial Enforcer
**STATUS: Creator-approved workouts and matched A/B scripts published in Review October 28, 2026. Filming and four actual Camera Tests pending.**

## Pokémon research, legality and training goal
- **National Pokédex:** #022 · **Species:** Fearow · **Type:** Normal / Flying.
- **Anatomy and behavior:** Beak Pokémon, Normal/Flying type. Evolves from Spearow at level 20; its long beak and wings allow decisive attacks. In the anime, the Spearow first encountered by Ash returns as a Fearow opposing Pidgeot.
- **Evolution:** Spearow → Fearow at level 20. This is a distinct two-stage family from Pidgey → Pidgeotto → Pidgeot.
- **Workout identity:** Turn Spearow's quick low-load beak strikes and reactive movement into stronger forward pressing, rotational power, lateral speed control and purposeful whole-body strength.
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate–Advanced · **Target duration:** 60–70 min including warm-up, rest and transitions · **Loaded intensity:** RPE 7–8.
- **Equipment:** Gym-approved medicine-ball rebound wall; soft medicine ball; properly anchored landmine and light barbell load; unobstructed shuffle/acceleration lane; cable machine and handle; trap bar with weights; dumbbells and chest-supported bench; open floor for reverse lunges.
- **Official learnset — Gen VII Ultra Sun/Ultra Moon:** Ultra Sun/Ultra Moon level-up: Aerial Ace Lv15 (Flying, inherited by evolution), Agility Lv27 (Psychic), Drill Peck Lv41 (Flying), Drill Run Lv45 (Ground). Drill Run is an actual move Fearow can learn by level-up; there is no need to claim an egg move or tutor method.
- **Narrative:** Spearow attacks Ash and Pikachu in anime episode 1; the Spearow reappears evolved as Fearow to oppose Pidgeot in "Pallet Party Panic!" This is anime continuity, not a claim that the featured attacks were shown in those episodes.

## Complete seven-exercise workout
| Order | Move / theme | Real gym exercise | Sets × repetitions or distance | Rest | Coaching note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Drill Run | **Rotational Medicine Ball Scoop Toss to Approved Wall** | 3 × 4/side | 90–120 sec | Throw from the hips while fresh into approved wall; control rebound and clear the lane. |
| 2 | Drill Peck | **Split-Stance Single-Arm Landmine Press** | 3 × 6/side | 120 sec | Legs steady, hand presses forward/up, full control on lowering; no hyperextension. |
| 3 | Agility | **Lateral Shuffle into Short Acceleration** | 3 × 2 × 4 m lateral + 5 m acceleration | 90 sec | Use the Pidgeotto drill silhouette: shuffle, plant gently and accelerate only into a clear lane. |
| 4 | Supporting | **Trap Bar Deadlift** | 3 × 5 | 150 sec | Ramp up load during warm-ups; working sets RPE 7–8 and no grinding. |
| 5 | Aerial Ace | **Standing Single-Arm High-to-Low Cable Sweep with Step** | 3 × 8/side | 90 sec | Smooth sweep, a small step and hip turn together; keep shoulders comfortable. |
| 6 | Supporting | **Chest-Supported Dumbbell Row** | 3 × 8 | 90 sec | Chest supported; controlled squeeze and complete return. |
| 7 | Supporting | **Reverse Lunge** | 3 × 8/side | 90 sec | Step backward, knee tracks over toes, maintain balance and a controlled pace. |

**Total:** 21 working sets. Warm-up/cooldown and warm-up ramp sets not counted. Session should be scheduled as a workout in itself; consecutive evolutions need not be trained on the same day.

### Warm-up
9–11 min: 3 minutes easy cardio; 8 air squats and 8 unloaded hinges; shoulder, trunk and hip mobility; 2 light lateral shuffle passes and submaximal accelerations; 6 light cable sweeps/side, 5 light landmine presses/side, 4 gentle rotational throws/side to an approved wall. Add 2–3 light trap-bar warm-up sets outside prescribed working sets.

### Cooldown
3–5 min: walk, reset normal breathing, comfortable shoulder and hip mobility. No forced neck rotation.

## Scaled difficulty
- **Beginner:** Replace medicine-ball wall throws with a low-load standing cable rotation; do a simple band forward press in place of the landmine press; lateral walk-shuffle without acceleration; kettlebell deadlift instead of trap bar; limit sets to 2 until movement quality is consistent.
- **Intermediate:** Use the prescribed low-moderate rotational medicine ball, light-to-moderate landmine and trap bar at RPE 7, full recovery between bursts, and limit agility speed to controllable effort.
- **Advanced:** Progress landmine/trap-bar load one small increment at a time while preserving bar path; use crisp throw speed without excessive load or repeated maximal attempts. Avoid programming heavy explosive throws immediately after fatigue.

## Safety
Only throw a soft medicine ball against a facility-approved wall with a clear landing/rebound area. No head-first drills, neck twisting or actual strikes. Properly anchor and test the landmine. Landmine press stays in a controlled forward-upward arc, not a neck thrust. Keep shuffle lane clear and allow controlled deceleration. Rotate from hips/feet, not lumbar spine; stop power sets when speed or alignment degrades.

## Connected evolution and rivalry
Spearow Peck band forward press → Fearow Drill Peck split-stance landmine press (same forward single-arm press family).
Spearow Aerial Ace cable wing sweep → Fearow Aerial Ace cable wing sweep plus deliberate step (same-name rule).
Spearow Pursuit cue acceleration → Fearow Agility lateral shuffle plus acceleration (related reaction/footwork themes, different named moves).
Spearow Fury Attack controlled alternating cable strikes → Fearow Drill Run medicine-ball rotation (different moves; thematic attack escalation, not a direct mechanical chain).
Cross-family check: Fearow Agility exactly matches Pidgeotto Agility's lateral-shuffle-to-acceleration family.
