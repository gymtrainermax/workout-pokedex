## Warm-up
8–10 min: 2–3 min easy cardio, 8 bodyweight squats and 8 hip hinges, shoulder circles/scapular movement, 10 sec gentle shadowboxing, a few plate-free Svend press rehearsals, light cable chop rotations and 10 sec small outside rope circles. Add 2–3 progressive trap-bar warm-up sets before working sets.

## Cooldown
3–5 min easy walking, calm breathing, and pain-free hip, shoulder and trunk mobility. No prolonged stretching through discomfort.

## Modifications
Beginner: high-handle trap-bar pulls; slower/shorter shadowboxing; unloaded palm squeeze instead of a plate; lighter chest-supported rows; supported split squat; light band or short-range cable chop; 10-sec smaller rope circles. Intermediate: follow the listed sets and loads near RPE 7–8 for deadlifts/rows/lunges and controlled moderate effort for accessories. Advanced: progress deadlift/row/lunge load modestly and refine punching/circular rope speed without turning technique into fatigue work.

## Safety
No actual fire or smoke; all flames/smoke are VFX. Ensure stable plate grip, keep plate light and below shoulder height, do not drop it or press through shoulder pain. Shadowbox the air with no dumbbells, no forced elbow lock or strike against equipment. Use a secured rope anchor and clear floor; circles remain controlled. For chops, pivot feet and hips, avoid forcing lumbar rotation. Stop sets when speed or form deteriorates. Load heavy strength movements with controlled RPE 7–8, not maximal effort.

## Exercise-specific technique notes
**Trap Bar Deadlift:** Primary strength movement first. Work around RPE 7–8 with controlled setup, full-foot pressure and no jerking. Beginner: high handles or kettlebell/block pull; advanced: incremental load without grinding.

**Shadowboxing:** Short crisp boxing combinations; balanced stance and light footwork. No opponent or impact, no handheld weights, no forced elbow lock. Beginner: slower 10–15 sec combinations; advanced: sharper 20 sec patterns without losing technique.

**Standing Svend Press:** Squeeze the plate at chest height and press forward without shrugging, locking elbows hard or arching the back. Choose a light plate that cannot slip; use a lightweight foam block for rehearsal. Beginner: empty-hand palm squeeze or light soft pad. This is a low-load accessory, not equivalent to a heavy chest press.

**Chest-Supported Dumbbell Row:** Keep chest supported, row elbows toward ribs, and avoid jerking or neck extension. Beginner: lighter dumbbells; advanced: progress load while keeping bench contact.

**Reverse Lunge:** Step back with a stable stance and lower within comfortable range. Beginner: supported stationary split squat; advanced: add light dumbbells if technique allows.

**Two-Handed High-to-Low Cable Woodchop:** Start high at one shoulder and chop diagonally to the opposite hip, allowing natural hip/foot pivot. Light-to-moderate controlled resistance. No ballistic lumbar twisting. Beginner: light band chop with small range; advanced: slightly more load without wrenching.

**Outside-Circle Battle Rope Waves:** Make simultaneous outward circles, creating waves that spread away from the body. Keep shoulders down, soft elbows, stable knees; stop for neck/shoulder discomfort. Beginner: smaller circles or shorter 10-sec intervals; advanced: larger controlled circles, not longer exhausting sets.
