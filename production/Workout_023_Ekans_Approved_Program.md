# Workout #023 — Ekans: Coil Foundations
**STATUS: Creator-approved exercise program and matched A/B scripts for public Review, October 28, 2026. Filming and all four actual Camera Tests pending; not Final.**

## Pokémon research and training identity
- **Species:** #023 Ekans, pure Poison type. Ekans is the Poison-type Snake Pokémon, a stealthy ground predator that coils and can swallow surprisingly large prey. It evolves into Arbok at level 22. Jessie’s Ekans in the anime eventually becomes Arbok.
- **Evolution:** Ekans → Arbok at level 22. Original Kanto forms, no alternate regional substitution.
- **Workout goal:** Build grounded rotational control, accurate forward pressing, safe gripping and steady anti-rotation bracing before Arbok develops stronger coiling power.
- **Difficulty:** Beginner+. **Session duration:** 40–50 minutes including transitions and rest. **Intensity:** RPE 6–7 for resistance movements; technical movements stay easy enough for crisp control.
- **Equipment:** Light resistance band and rated secure anchor; Light second band and rated anchor; Goblet dumbbell; Rubber-coated training plate; Dumbbells; Chest-supported incline bench; Cable column or rated band and mat.
- **Move legality:** Ultra Sun/Ultra Moon (Gen VII) level-up: Wrap Lv1 (Normal), Poison Sting Lv4 (Poison), Bite Lv9 (Dark), Glare Lv12 (Normal). No egg-move or tutor exceptions.
- **Training context:** One full-body session per day as appropriate, not an instruction to train both evolutions back-to-back. Avoid unfamiliar loads and get local coaching where needed.

## Complete seven-exercise workout
| Order | Move alias | Real gym exercise | Sets × dose | Rest | Coaching cues |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wrap | **Standing Resistance-Band Trunk Rotation** | 3 × 8/side | 75 sec | Pivot feet and hips together; avoid wrenching lower back; light resistance. |
| 2 | Poison Sting | **Split-Stance Single-Arm Resistance-Band Punch** | 3 × 8/side | 75 sec | Secure anchor, smooth forward punch and return; no contact. |
| 3 | Supporting | **Goblet Squat** | 2 × 8–10 | 90 sec | Ground whole foot and squat to a comfortable depth. |
| 4 | Bite | **Rubber-Coated Plate Pinch Hold** | 2 × 20 sec | 60 sec | Use a rubber-coated plate; keep it near thigh height, avoid toe hazards. |
| 5 | Supporting | **Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift** | 2 × 8–10 | 90 sec | Controlled hip hinge with soft knees, dumbbells close to legs. |
| 6 | Supporting | **Chest-Supported Dumbbell Row** | 2 × 10 | 75 sec | Chest stays on bench, row smoothly without shrugging. |
| 7 | Glare | **Tall-Kneeling Pallof Press with Two-Second Hold** | 2 × 8/side | 60–75 sec | Brace and breathe through a 2-second extension pause; avoid flaring ribs. |

**Total training volume:** 16 working sets. Warm-up and cooldown are not counted.

### Warm-up
7–8 minutes: 2 minutes easy walking/cycling, 8 comfortable air squats, 8 unloaded hinges, 6 slow torso turns with feet pivoting, gentle standing band presses, 2 short 10-second grip rehearsals and 6 light Pallof press holds per side. Warm-up practice is not counted toward working sets.

### Cooldown
3–5 minutes easy walking and comfortable upper-back, hip and wrist mobility; avoid aggressive neck stretching or simulated constriction.

## Beginner / intermediate / advanced modifications
**Beginner:** use very light band resistance, a rubber-coated 1–2.5 kg training plate or a soft foam disc, box squat instead of deep goblet squat, and an easy tall-kneeling Pallof hold of 10–15 seconds if pressing is difficult.
**Intermediate:** increase band tension or plate weight slightly; keep 2–3 good reps in reserve and maintain smooth rotation.
**Advanced:** use as a light technique/anti-rotation day; do not convert band twists or grip holds to max-effort tests.

## Safety and pre-filming Camera Tests
Anchor bands to a tested and rated fixed structure, never an unsecured rack attachment. For Wrap, pivot hips and feet together and use a light band; avoid twisting under load through the lower back. Keep every grip implement away from the mouth and face. Poison Sting is an empty-space band press, not a strike against a person. Glare represents a steady, confident brace—not breath-holding, choking, neck extension or staring into a bright light. Exhale on effort and stop if dizzy or painful.
Every signature move has Pokémon, Trainer, and **pre-filming feasibility** checks. The four actual filmed Camera Tests must be captured and reviewed before any status changes to Final. No filmed test is claimed passed.

## Paired evolution direction
- **Ekans Wrap → Arbok Coil:** band rotation → loaded controlled landmine 180s.
- **Ekans Poison Sting → Arbok Gunk Shot:** light one-arm forward band punch → two-hand forward medicine-ball chest pass to approved wall.
- **Ekans Bite → Arbok Crunch:** rubber-coated plate pinch hold → short rubber-coated plate pinch carry.
- **Ekans Glare → Arbok Glare:** tall-kneeling Pallof press hold → standing Pallof press step-out with identical anti-rotation intent.
