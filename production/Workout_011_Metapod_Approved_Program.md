# Workout #011 — Metapod: Shell Protocol

**Status: Creator-approved October 27, 2026.** Exercises and matched A/B scripts published as **Review**. Filming, four actual Camera Tests and A/B results pending; not Final.

## Pokémon research and training identity

- **National Pokédex:** #011 · **Type:** Bug · **Stage:** Cocoon · **Counterpart:** #014 Kakuna.
- **Lore and ability:** Cocoon Pokémon, Bug type; Shed Skin. It protects a soft body by remaining comparatively motionless. The workout becomes quieter and more bracing-focused than Caterpie.
- **Relationship:** Metapod develops a protective shell; Kakuna creates a taller, sharper guard. Both pivot toward Harden without inventing cocoon-exclusive attack arsenals.
- **Workout goal:** Deliberate cocoon stability, body tension, low-speed inherited skills and purposeful light strength.
- **Level:** Beginner+ / Recovery-Strength · **Planned duration:** 35–45 minutes including setup/rest/transitions. · **Intensity:** Deload relative to larval conditioning: easy-to-moderate RPE 5–6, always breathe through holds.
- **Equipment:** Exercise mat, cable column or secure band alternative, light soft sandbag, sturdy squat box, light dumbbells, unobstructed farmer-carry lane, solid wall.
- **Evolution chain:** Caterpie → Metapod → Butterfree. **Opposing line:** Weedle → Kakuna → Beedrill.

## Complete seven-exercise workout

| Order | Move | Real exercise | Sets × dose | Rest | Key coaching cue |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Harden | Forearm Plank | **3 × 15–20 sec** | 60–75 sec | Squeeze glutes lightly, elbows under shoulders, short breaths, finish before shaking. |
| 2 | String Shot | Half-Kneeling Single-Arm Cable Press with Pause | **2 × 8/side, 2-sec pause** | 75 sec | Single-arm cable press from stable half-kneel; keep pelvis/ribs aligned without torso turn. |
| 3 | Supporting | Goblet Box Squat | **2 × 8** | 90 sec | Controlled sit to sturdy box and stand using full feet; keep effort gentle. |
| 4 | Bug Bite | Sandbag Bear-Hug Hold | **2 × 25–30 sec** | 75 sec | Light soft bag in front of ribs; steady breath and upright balance; not heavier by default. |
| 5 | Supporting | Chest-Supported Dumbbell Row | **2 × 10** | 75 sec | Supported row with 2-sec lowering and no shrug. |
| 6 | Supporting | Farmer Carry | **2 × 20 m** | 90 sec | Carry two light weights with level shoulders and unobstructed walk. |
| 7 | Tackle | Paused Light Sled Push | **2 × 8 m (2-sec pause every 2 m)** | 75 sec | Use a nearly empty sled with stable handles; pause every 2 m for 2 seconds, keep steady breathing and never lock joints. |

**Total:** 15 prescribed sets across seven exercises. **Signature-move sets:** 9. The sets shown are training prescriptions; additional warm-up rehearsals are not included.

### Warm-up

6–8 min easy walk/bike and gentle rib/shoulder, hip and ankle mobility; light half-kneeling cable rehearsals, unloaded farmer carry and easy wall-drive lean.

### Cooldown

3–5 min easy walk and quiet nasal or comfortable breathing; no prolonged forced holds.

### Regressions / progressions

- Beginner: elevated forearm plank, light band press from seated posture, empty sandbag or pillow hug, supported chair squat, very light suitcase carry.
- Intermediate: use listed short holds and 2-second press pauses while preserving easy RPE.
- Advanced: treat this as a deliberate low-stress technique or recovery-strength day; resist the urge to maximize hold times.

### Equipment and safety checks

Metapod is not asked to crawl quickly or train to failure. Plank and bear-hug holds should never require breath-holding. Stable platform for box squats; clear farmer-carry lane. Inherited moves do not mean Metapod naturally learns four new attacks.

- Keep signature technique full-body and legible with **no special effects** on the first Camera Test; a clean filming rehearsal is possible but has not happened yet.
- Work on stable dry flooring; provide full recovery between faster or power movements. The entire session need not be completed by beginners if they require regressions.
- This is fitness-inspired entertainment, not real contact combat or a guarantee of effects from a Pokémon status move.
