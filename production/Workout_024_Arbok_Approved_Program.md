# Workout #024 — Arbok: Cobra Command
**STATUS: Creator-approved exercise program and matched A/B scripts for public Review, October 28, 2026. Filming and all four actual Camera Tests pending; not Final.**

## Pokémon research and training identity
- **Species:** #024 Arbok, pure Poison type. Arbok is the Poison-type Cobra Pokémon, evolving from Ekans at level 22. Its hood markings resemble a frightening face, and its natural hunting style combines intimidation and coiling. Jessie’s Arbok evolved from her Ekans in the anime episode ‘Dig Those Diglett’.
- **Evolution:** Ekans → Arbok at level 22. Original Kanto forms, no alternate regional substitution.
- **Workout goal:** Develop Ekans’s rotation into loaded landmine control, its Bite grip into a demanding carry, its Poison Sting projection into a safe med-ball chest pass, and its Glare stability into moving anti-rotation.
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate+. **Session duration:** 55–65 minutes including transitions and rest. **Intensity:** RPE 7–8 for resistance movements; technical movements stay easy enough for crisp control.
- **Equipment:** Soft light medicine ball and venue-approved rebound wall; Landmine unit fixed in rated base, barbell and light plate; Trap bar and weights in a clear lane; Rubber-coated training plates and clear carry path; Incline bench and dumbbells; Incline bench and dumbbells; Single cable station or rated secure anti-rotation band anchor.
- **Move legality:** Ultra Sun/Ultra Moon (Gen VII): Crunch learned immediately upon evolving from Ekans (Dark); Glare level 12 / retained (Normal); Coil level 56 (Poison); Gunk Shot level 63 (Poison), also available as a USUM move-tutor move. Later-level moves are legitimate Arbok moves even if not available immediately at evolution.
- **Training context:** One full-body session per day as appropriate, not an instruction to train both evolutions back-to-back. Avoid unfamiliar loads and get local coaching where needed.

## Complete seven-exercise workout
| Order | Move alias | Real gym exercise | Sets × dose | Rest | Coaching cues |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Gunk Shot | **Medicine Ball Chest Pass to Approved Wall** | 3 × 5 | 90–120 sec | Use soft ball to approved rebound wall only; throw from standing without neck/head movement; allow controlled rebound. |
| 2 | Coil | **Standing Landmine 180s** | 3 × 8 alternating sweeps total | 90 sec | Light landmine, controlled hip/foot turns, no low-back twisting. |
| 3 | Supporting | **Trap Bar Deadlift** | 3 × 5 | 150 sec | Keep 1–3 reps in reserve, controlled hinge and full rest. |

| 5 | Supporting | **Chest-Supported Dumbbell Row** | 3 × 8 | 90 sec | Full chest support and slow return, maintain shoulder control. |
| 6 | Supporting | **Incline Dumbbell Bench Press** | 3 × 8 | 90–120 sec | Stable feet, controlled lowering, no bouncing. |
| 7 | Glare | **Standing Pallof Press with Step-Out and Two-Second Hold** | 3 × 8/side | 75–90 sec | Small controlled steps, ribs stacked, hold 2 seconds at extension while breathing. |

**Total training volume:** 21 working sets. Warm-up and cooldown are not counted.

### Warm-up
9–10 minutes: 3 minutes easy cardio, 8 air squats, 8 unloaded hinges, 6 band rotations per side, 4 very light medicine-ball chest-pass rehearsals (without throwing until cleared), 6 light Pallof presses each side, and 2–3 progressively heavier trap-bar deadlift warm-up sets. Warm-up sets are not part of the 21 working sets.

### Cooldown
4–5 minutes relaxed walking and gentle shoulder/hip/wrist mobility with normal breathing. No neck straining or maximal stretch.

## Beginner / intermediate / advanced modifications
**Beginner:** light soft-ball chest pass without wall rebound or skip power throws; standing light-band trunk rotation in place of landmine 180s; two hands on a small rubber-coated plate for short pinch carries; kettlebell deadlift instead of trap bar; shorter step-out Pallof hold.
**Intermediate:** follow 21-set prescription using RPE 7–8 for the deadlift and presses, lighter loads for rotation and throws; keep two repetitions in reserve for upper body.
**Advanced:** increase landmine and deadlift loads conservatively across separate sessions; med-ball throws remain fast and technically clean, never heavy or maximal volume.

## Safety and pre-filming Camera Tests
Use only a gym-approved wall and soft medicine ball for chest passes, standing sufficiently far back to receive rebounds under control. An unsecured wall or crowded lane means substituting a controlled cable press. Never throw actual liquids, powders, or waste for Gunk Shot. Verify landmine attachment, keep the bar’s path below shoulder strain range, pivot feet/hips in Coil, and do not wrench the lumbar spine. Plate carries must be rubber-coated and kept away from toes; stop if grip slips. Glare is a Pallof anti-rotation drill, not breath-holding or neck loading. Deadlifts use a neutral comfortable hinge and rack-safe weights; stop on form breakdown.
Every signature move has Pokémon, Trainer, and **pre-filming feasibility** checks. The four actual filmed Camera Tests must be captured and reviewed before any status changes to Final. No filmed test is claimed passed.

## Paired evolution direction
- **Ekans Wrap → Arbok Coil:** band rotation → loaded controlled landmine 180s.
- **Ekans Poison Sting → Arbok Gunk Shot:** light one-arm forward band punch → two-hand forward medicine-ball chest pass to approved wall.
- **Ekans Bite → Arbok Crunch:** rubber-coated plate pinch hold → short rubber-coated plate pinch carry.
- **Ekans Glare → Arbok Glare:** tall-kneeling Pallof press hold → standing Pallof press step-out with identical anti-rotation intent.
- **Anime note:** Jessie's Ekans evolves into Arbok in “Dig Those Diglett.” The evolution relationship, unlike the *move* arrows, is canon.
