HOW THE DOTS GET CONNECTED

Evidence before vibes.

Similarity is interesting. It is not automatically proof of copying. Every connection is framed according to what the evidence can actually support.

01

Start with the source

What is the underlying animal, plant, fossil, myth, object, body plan or mascot role?

02

Compare what had to match

A snowflake will be symmetrical. A mantis already has blade-like forelegs. Some similarities are built into the source.

03

Find what did not have to match

Color placement, face geometry, proportions, role and unusual accessories are where a resemblance becomes more interesting.

04

Check the timeline

Who appeared first? Chronology can establish precedent, but it does not prove direct inspiration by itself.

05

Look for the bridge

Shared designers, companies, official lore, interviews and documented variants can turn a resemblance into an actual historical connection.

06

Label the conclusion

Confirmed connection, documented echo, possible influence, convergence, shared source or simply a great comparison.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Built by a fan who kept noticing things.

Creature Connections began with Tamagotchi Paradise and a simple question: why does this character remind me of something from another franchise? That question grew into a catalog spanning virtual pets, monster games, anime, card games, folklore, zoology and character-design history.

The goal is not to flatten every character into “they look alike.” It is to make the source material visible—and to appreciate the design choices that make each interpretation its own thing.
